# Gift and exchange

The name is rendered **Jan Savos / Yansavos** in the recording; **Yan Savos** is a provisional display form. He is described as a Forged from Rios who brought powerful resurrection magic to Eok’s leaders. He declined public acclaim and asked in exchange for vessels capable of traveling to the upper reef and beyond, with no expectation of returning.[^s005]

The Court agreed after deliberation, developed the requested skyship fleet, and tested the resurrection magic before opening the first plot. The explicit clarification is that this decision was deliberative, rather than made through divination. He departed into the astral sea with **nine dreadnought-scale vessels** and was not heard from again in the account supplied.[^s005]

# Reputation in Rios

The Drifters discuss his unclear motives and the Pillars’ restrictions on welcoming him into Dochas Ùr. They distinguish mystery from proven malice. The conversation explicitly separates the players’ previous-campaign knowledge from what these characters know. His actual motives, current fate and any connection to the party’s present crisis remain unknown.[^s005]
