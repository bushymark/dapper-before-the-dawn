# Session 2 — before the Drifters

Bernard von Kepler is Whipperwill’s recently blinded captain and Haskel’s master. The captain’s ship, recent blindness and master relationship with Haskel in Session 2 connect him to the later astronomer, resolving the earlier identity question. His exact name spelling remains provisional. He refuses to let Elias tinker with the engine and entrusts his custody to Haskel. Their relationship includes concern for Haskel’s independence as a Forged.[^s002-identity]

The subsequent boarding account is preserved at [Whipperwill’s captain](wickerwill-captain.md).

# Intervention at Alora

**Bernard von Kepler** is the spoken name of a blind astronomer whom Haskel recognizes, arriving from Resolute’s direction. He carries a staff sometimes used as a cane. He hears Haskel describe and communicate his Astral Sea experience, then promises what aid he can and to speak to the Court. He cannot guarantee safety; he asks Haskel to help keep the others safe by remaining with them. He objects to discovery and destruction going hand in hand, calls for the general to stop and eventually orders the party to escape.

His name’s exact spelling remains unconfirmed. The scene fits the honored Bernard mentioned in Session 6; his role as [Whipperwill’s captain](wickerwill-captain.md) is established by the earlier Session 2 voyage.[^s002-identity]

# Open consequences

He has not pardoned the group or guaranteed protection. Future contact and the Court’s response to his testimony remain unknown.

# Session 8 — refuge and a shared course

The GM recap calls Bernard Haskel’s closest friend, while Haskel repeatedly calls him master. Haskel knows to seek a future sending stone through Heaven Sent. The narration recalls Bernard vetoing Haskel’s telescopic vision upgrade and losing his own sight a few months earlier. Haskel had interpreted the refusal as bitterness after that loss, but the motive is not confirmed. Bernard’s influence is a reason for hope, not a pardon, guaranteed safety or a message actually received. The earlier Session 2 voyage establishes that he is Whipperwill’s captain.[^s002-identity][^s008-yt]
