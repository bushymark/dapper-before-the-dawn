# Identity

The primer names **High Archivist Paedan Dahhm**, a diplomat from Eok who met the remaining Pillar and sought a Library exchange decades earlier.[^primer]

# Court leadership

Upon arrival, the party asked to speak with **Therus**, the current leader of the Court of Conscience. Therus is **Paedan Dahhm’s son and successor**: Paedan had led the Court previously and had interacted with the Pillar Morgaen many years earlier. This establishes the recalled succession and relationship, not that the party had already met Therus.[^arrival-dictation]

[Court](../factions/court-of-conscience.md), [Therus](therus.md).

# Information obtained during the audience

The party learned that Therus had turned in his parents and that they were serving time. Therus resented Paedan’s love for Rios. The charges, sentence and parents’ version of events are unknown.[^audience-dictation]
