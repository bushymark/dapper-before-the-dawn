# Source-supported identity

The primer names **High Archivist Paedan Dahhm**, a diplomat from Eok who met the remaining Pillar and sought a Library exchange decades earlier.[^primer]

# Later user clarification

Upon arrival, the party asked to speak with **Therus**, the current leader of the Court of Conscience. The user identifies him as **Paedan Dahhm’s son and successor**: Paedan had led the Court previously and had interacted with the Pillar Morgaen many years earlier. This establishes the recalled succession and relationship, not that the party had already met Therus.[^arrival-dictation]

The dictated forms “Padan Dahn” and “Padan” are matched contextually to Paedan Dahhm by the earlier Eok/Pillar diplomatic encounter. This does not retroactively settle every garbled statement in the original transcript.

[Court](../factions/court-of-conscience.md), [Therus](therus.md).

[^primer]: Preserved Drifters primer, PDF page 13, Library of Ioun account; names Paedan Dahhm as High Archivist and diplomat.

[^arrival-dictation]: User retrospective note, heading “arrival, Court leadership and airship captain”: paragraphs 1–2 (Therus, Paedan and Morgaen), paragraph 3 (reported gods, city levels, Maochao and species observed).

# Information obtained during the audience

The party learned that Therus had turned in his parents and that they were serving time. Therus resented Paedan’s love for Rios. The charges, sentence and parents’ version of events are unknown.[^audience-dictation]
