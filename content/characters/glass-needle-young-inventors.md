# A shared invention

**Finn**, nicknamed **Prince Finn**, **Tunky**, a red-haired tiefling girl, and a **white-haired boy** jointly develop a material that grows with heat and shrinks in the cold. Their exact name spellings remain provisional; the white-haired boy’s name is especially unclear. The royal nickname does not establish Finn as a political ruler. They initially nominate Finn as their one school applicant, believing all three will not get places. Their work has taken many iterations and is not solely his achievement.[^s002-children]

The white-haired boy’s father died in a mine collapse; protecting miners from such accidents is one purpose envisioned for the material. Finn’s family makes room for his laboratory despite limited space at home. Tunky also values history and literature, which Ustalav dismisses in favor of the hard sciences.[^s002-children]

# Rescue and opportunity

All three survive the platform accident, with Haskel rescuing Finn and Elias, Dayne and Shamus intervening as the professor and another child are endangered. Finn loses his crown into Lower Lastings. Dayne offers a route to presenting their work and receiving scholarships; Elias makes their opportunity central to his own teaching offer. They leave aboard Whipperwill toward the school, delighted at the prospect of attending together.[^s002-rescue][^s002-offer][^s002-departure]

# The cold child

The white-haired boy carries a snow globe made by his father. Its imperfect magic continually takes heat from him. Koris accepts temporary responsibility for the globe and promises its return; the child warms as she becomes cold. A suggestion of vampire ancestry does not establish the cause of his symptoms.[^s002-globe]

[Session 2](../sessions/session-002-citizens.md) precedes their later airship ordeal. All three eventually return safely to their original bodies in [Session 4](../events/session-004-physical-return.md).[^s004] The earlier loss of Finn’s crown is not evidence of its later recovery.
