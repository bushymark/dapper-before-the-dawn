# Identity

**Dayne** is a player character in [The Citizens](../factions/the-citizens.md).[^s003-roster]

# Session 2 — before the Drifters

Dayne represents Headmistress Vesper in a Glass Needle faculty interview. He helps Koris remain in Lastings by transferring a week of Lifespan, de-escalates Shamus’s confrontation and helps rescue the professor from the platform. He rejects Ustalav’s teaching approach and offers Elias a position, subject to Court arrangements, while opening a scholarship route for the three young inventors and Koris. The group departs for the school aboard Whipperwill.[^s002-road][^s002-arrival][^s002-grimm][^s002-rescue][^s002-offer]

# Adventures

Dayne eventually stabilizes the wheel, helps redirect the damaged airship away from the city toward northern forest, and assists Koris across a gap. His briefcase is readied as an improvised weapon. Later in the cosmic passage, he shapes a starlight harpoon.[^s003]

# Shared transition

The combined group experiences narrated death and continued awareness as objects in a cosmic state. It remains unclear this character’s exact object form or a return to a living body.[^s003]

# Session 4 development

Dayne’s thrown spear fails to embed, but he later synchronizes the vessel with the whale and helps Cole climb. His physical form is reflective metal based on a wedding ring, with sapphire veins/tendons and blank white eyes. He mediates the blame dispute and identifies Alora Valley as a likely, one-day northbound body-recovery lead. The lead has not been visited.[^s004]

# Session 5 — competing duties

Dayne comforts Koris, discusses the Court and his family obligations, and participates in the body-recovery planning. He insists that pursuit of answers must not threaten his wife and children. He later voices support for the group as recent events disturb his former security. His discussion of [plots](../lore/resurrection-plots-and-victus.md) supplies important cultural context, but does not mean every proposed restoration method has been tested.[^s005]

# Session 6 — preparations before dawn

Dayne offers to take responsibility for Elias and Haskel, but Haskel will not transfer custody. Dayne wants to return to his children; his academy position can influence important listeners without placing him above Court rules. He joins Cole, Haskel and Elias on recovery, with loading the bodies among his proposed tasks. His scholarly importance does not establish a formal Court rank or a completed employment arrangement.[^s006]

# Session 7 — escape and return

Dayne exchanges respect for scholarship and dialogue with Maren, then stands beside Cole to defend Haskel and Elias. An improvised flaming torch takes on purpose in his grip. The general suplexes him through a greenhouse; Dayne responds with a powerful critical strike that breaks the torch. He is subsequently wounded unconscious and carried as leverage, stabilizes, and receives five vitality from Emeric. The last claw swipe leaves him barely conscious. Elias’s lynx helps him reach the cart. He returns to an older human body of poised bearing, with black hair and silver streaks; his wedding-ring victus and pocket watch are recovered. Contacting his family remains urgent and unresolved.[^s007]

# Session 8 — refuge and a shared course

Dayne notices unidentified red scales behind the Last Ember and discovers the smith’s bone ash in the forge, without immediately showing the remains to everyone. He successfully prepares the shared meal after a reroll. His wife’s letter is missing and his recovered wedding ring is damaged. He insists on protecting and contacting his wife and two children while supporting the Drifters’ mission. His family lives in Lastings. No family message is sent, rescue arranged or contact confirmed.[^s008-yt]

# Session 9 — repairs and Rustwater Mine

Dayne gently approaches the detached ship’s wheel, lashes it to his arm and earns the living vessel’s recognition as its pilot. In a private moment the wood bends toward him and begins growing. His lunchbox is accounted for, though his wife’s letters remain missing. He takes a mining pick and later finds the iron seam and helps the group gather ore, staying with the main group while Maren and Haskel scout. Therica secures his promise to try an apple pie in exchange for keeping watch; no pie is made in this session.[^s009-supplies][^s009-repairs][^s009-mine][^s009-theory]
