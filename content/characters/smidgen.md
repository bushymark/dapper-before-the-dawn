# Smidgen and her home

**Smidgen** introduces herself as the town’s **patchwork**. Her name is source-supported but has not been explicitly spelled; the duties of “patchwork” remain undefined. She is a gnome with large glasses, a cottage, vegetable supplies, an apple press, beehives and painting materials in [Alora Valley](../places/alora-valley.md). She rotates her worship through the pantheon alphabetically rather than describing one permanent patron.[^s005]

# Shelter near the celebration

Smidgen spots the party near the Court’s gathering and calls them inside. She splashes turpentine around her doorway and covers windows with a patchwork cloak. When an archivist suitor knocks, she conceals the guests. She wants them to leave promptly and keep any confrontation away from her property; her help does not imply support for every part of their plan.[^s005]

She says [the White Fang’s captain](general-maochao.md) is celebrating retirement while insisting on catching the people he pursues. The gathering has taken over a visiting theater troupe’s performance area, and corpses and recovered goods stand by the stage. She points the party toward the troupe’s remaining supplies and notes that officials had already sought a play celebrating the general’s successes.[^s005]

# Supplies and next steps

She provides **five sausage links** for the starving wolves the party hopes to help. The group offers small household chores in return. Smidgen watches part of the patrol leave toward the forest while the party debates surrender and plans a theatrical [body-recovery heist](../quests/recover-the-original-bodies.md). They remain in her shelter during that planning; no performed play or completed recovery follows in this session.[^s005]
