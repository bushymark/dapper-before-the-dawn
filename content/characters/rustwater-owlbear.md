# A prisoner on the water

The party sees this gaunt owlbear across Rustwater Mine’s underground lake at the end of Session 9. It has yellow eyes and a black beak, is soaked in blood, and appears desperately hungry. It walks on the water’s surface. A heavy rusted chain bites into its neck and descends toward an unseen weight below.[^s009-owlbear]

A small stone or light orbits just above its head. The creature reacts to disturbances and tries unsuccessfully to remain hidden. Neither the orbiting object’s function nor the means keeping it on the water is established. It is not identified as a guardian beast.[^s009-owlbear]
