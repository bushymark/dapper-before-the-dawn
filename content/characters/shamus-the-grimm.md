# A quarrel settled

Shamus the Grimm is a strong, tattooed visitor from Rios who feels watched and out of place in Lastings. His exact name spelling remains unconfirmed. Dayne meets him threatening a shopkeeper over what he takes as an insult. As gorgons approach, Dayne persuades him to release the merchant. A meal and help traveling toward a Rios community near the First Woods ease his anger. His criticisms of Lastings express his own experience.[^s002-grimm]

He later rushes to help during the platform accident, alongside Dayne. They part on friendly terms; he says the earlier favor is still owed. He does not join the Citizens’ school-bound voyage.[^s002-rescue][^s002-grimm]
