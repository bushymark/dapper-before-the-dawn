# The blacksmith’s account

[Recknar](mysterious-blacksmith.md) says the Bloom’s leader first came with a walking, flower-marked tree about ten years earlier. A wooden humanoid with those same markings returned years later, went below the mines with companions, and came back alone. He killed Recknar’s wife and two children, called himself **Tallow**, and invited Recknar to seek revenge.[^s005]

The smith identifies this figure with the earlier tree. That recognition does not independently establish Tallow’s transformation, ancestry, connection to a particular dryad or current location. The name’s spelling remains provisional. Whatever the Bloom wanted below the mines remains unknown.[^s005]

[Cole](cole-buckit.md) offers to avenge the family if he encounters Tallow. No such meeting or revenge is completed.[^s005]
