# Session 2 — before the Drifters

Poppy accompanies Koris on her first visit to Lastings, prevents a dangerous jump toward an elevator and later bites Ustalav in the neck during his struggle over the children’s vial. The attack contributes to the platform crisis; no one dies. Poppy subsequently helps stow the snow globe Koris has promised to care for.[^s002-arrival][^s002-rescue][^s002-globe]

[Citizens-only Session 2](../sessions/session-002-citizens.md).

# Observations

[Koris](koris.md) calls Swamp Poppy out of a bag in a dark, nightmarish manifestation. She instructs it to protect the children without harming anyone, but it attacks Cole. Its crocodilian face appears to melt and reform against Cole’s heat.[^s003]

Maren’s psychic attack encounters no centralized brain and a presence of overwhelming scale. The GM rules psychic immunity for this encounter. These observations do not establish ancestry, full statistics or an identity with a guardian beast. It is distinct from Elias’s blue feline construct.[^s003]

[Airship disaster](../events/session-003-airship-disaster.md). Name spelling remains provisional.

# Session 4 development

Swamp Poppy is initially absent/unmanifested after death; Koris uses the full manifestation action and the shadow resolves into a floating green crocodilian eidolon. It is involved in tail-strike damage sharing and the stillness scene. After the return it emerges from bushes as a floppy stuffed alligator. Exact permanent form, nature and canonical spelling remain unresolved.[^s004]

# Session 7 — escape and return

Swamp Poppy accompanies Koris into the Alora fight, nonlethally subdues the shield guard, then attacks the general. As the gorgon partly petrifies the general’s leg, Poppy dismisses rather than sharing that effect. The GM excludes Poppy from Emeric’s bonding ability; this ruling is scoped to the ability, not a statement about the souls of all companions.[^s007]

# Session 8 — refuge and a shared course

The GM recap and player clarify that Poppy is an alligator rather than a crocodile. At the forge Poppy wanders toward the water; a splash, swan cry and silence are heard, but no explicit feeding outcome is narrated. Poppy later comforts Koris as she checks her objects. This is not a new death or permanent loss of the companion.[^s008-yt]
