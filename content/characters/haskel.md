# Identity

**Haskel** is a player character in [The Citizens](../factions/the-citizens.md).[^s003-roster]

# Adventures

Haskel attempts to control the vessel, channels elemental power and heals the injured captain. Near the engines he works with Elias. After the gap, he forms a starlight chain for the harpoon and whale towing. Haskell/Pascal variants are matched by continuing role and context, not accepted as aliases.[^s003]

# Shared transition

The combined group experiences narrated death and continued awareness as objects in a cosmic state. It remains unclear this character’s exact object form or a return to a living body.[^s003]

# Session 4 development

Haskel moves and heals the ship with kinesis/infusion, identifies Oma’s regeneration and possible song threat, proposes a bridle and completes the gentle lattice. His remanifested form is angular blue/black obsidian-like material with a detached compass-globe head. He says guardian beasts are celestial phenomena not owned by anyone; this is his assertion rather than confirmation of the impact’s intent.[^s004]

# Session 5 — authority and custody

Haskel maintains low gravity during travel. A legal assessment distinguishes the Citizens’ bystander status from the seizure attempt, while his own exposure may depend on making amends and someone senior vouching for him. At the forge, Koris privately tells him of blood and a cursed object. He invokes Court authority, obtains access to the basement and accepts custody of the [wrapped blade](../lore/blacksmiths-cursed-blade.md). His confidence falters on hearing that the Court failed to protect the smith’s family. He explains that his station favors truthfulness; he is not established as magically unable to lie.[^s005]
