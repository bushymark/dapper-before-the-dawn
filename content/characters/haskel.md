# Identity

**Haskel** is a player character in [The Citizens](../factions/the-citizens.md).[^s003-roster]

# Session 2 — before the Drifters

Haskel accompanies the newly released Elias under Bernard von Kepler’s supervision, with responsibility for his custody. He asks the captain about allowing work on the engine, but the request is denied. During the platform accident he pulls Finn to safety. He witnesses the faculty discussions for Court purposes and notes that any appointment for Elias needs proper documentation.[^s002-ship][^s002-rescue][^s002-offer]

[Citizens-only Session 2](../sessions/session-002-citizens.md).

# Adventures

Haskel attempts to control the vessel, channels elemental power and heals the injured captain. Near the engines he works with Elias. After the gap, he forms a starlight chain for the harpoon and whale towing. Haskell/Pascal variants are matched by continuing role and context, not accepted as aliases.[^s003]

# Shared transition

The combined group experiences narrated death and continued awareness as objects in a cosmic state. It remains unclear this character’s exact object form or a return to a living body.[^s003]

# Session 4 development

Haskel moves and heals the ship with kinesis/infusion, identifies Oma’s regeneration and possible song threat, proposes a bridle and completes the gentle lattice. His remanifested form is angular blue/black obsidian-like material with a detached compass-globe head. He says guardian beasts are celestial phenomena not owned by anyone; this is his assertion rather than confirmation of the impact’s intent.[^s004]

# Session 5 — authority and custody

Haskel maintains low gravity during travel. A legal assessment distinguishes the Citizens’ bystander status from the seizure attempt, while his own exposure may depend on making amends and someone senior vouching for him. At the forge, Koris privately tells him of blood and a cursed object. He invokes Court authority, obtains access to the basement and accepts custody of the [wrapped blade](../lore/blacksmiths-cursed-blade.md). His confidence falters on hearing that the Court failed to protect the smith’s family. He explains that his station favors truthfulness; he is not established as magically unable to lie.[^s005]

# Session 6 — preparations before dawn

Haskel insists Elias remain under his supervision and refuses Dayne’s offer to take over. He fears imprisonment and losing his hard-won station, yet agrees that the bodies should be returned before proceedings. He privately questions Elias’s intentions while allowing the enlarged diversion to proceed despite its legal risk. He agrees to open the coffins on a promise of no further murder. Possible refuge in Rios is discussed, conditional on avoiding war; no relocation or surrender occurs.[^s006]

# Session 7 — escape and return

Haskel teaches Elias the coffin-opening gesture with a requested promise against spreading violence or evil. He resists the general’s demand to surrender, attacks with gravity/force, and warns Cole about the general’s counters. He retrieves his compass victus and shares his Astral Sea experience with Bernard von Kepler, who promises limited aid and Court testimony without guaranteed safety. Haskel escapes a gorgon sweep through a low-gravity swimming motion. At dawn his original featureless metallic Forged body returns, with solid starlight-blue eyes; he dons a discarded theater wig and hides behind his high collar. He remains with the others, not pardoned or released from all duties.[^s007]

# Session 8 — refuge and a shared course

Haskel sees an opportunity for relative safety in the woods and hopes Bernard’s influence can help them return to lawful life. A message could be left through Heaven Sent, but collecting it would risk entering a city. He objects to hasty victus severance, admits uncertainty about how much of the group Bernard could protect, and shares restricted cosmological knowledge as a gesture of good faith. He believes the sun is made of living salamanders and dispersed to dodge the meteor; the other Citizens do not all know this. He accepts continued information-seeking alongside the group’s preliminary repair/investigation course; no surrender or city visit occurs.[^s008-yt]
