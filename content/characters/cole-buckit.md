# Identity

Cole Buckit is played by Wild.[^notes] In the supplied scene Cole is described as 9½ feet tall, appearing as flame, plasma, and lightning with visible armor beneath; physical handling of small objects is difficult.[^transcript] This description does not establish an ancestry, class, or complete rules build.

# September 28 recorded scene

Cole notices [Sledge](sledge.md) manhandling an [expectant father from Eok](eokian-expectant-father.md), follows them into an alley, and approaches to de-escalate. A Diplomacy roll is reported as a natural 20, total 30. Cole challenges assumptions about the man's culture and gives the man a small desired trinket, handling it carefully. The transcript says Cole's presence prevents escalation, but Sledge still refuses to release the man at that moment.[^transcript]

The excerpt ends while the ritual and festival naming are being discussed. Later user recollection supplies the outcome: Cole spoke to Sledge about strange practices in Dochas Ùr and Rios, resolving the encounter peaceably, though Sledge was not necessarily happy.[^sledge-dictation] See [session recap](../sessions/2026-09-28-drifters-s001.md).

# Kite festival

Emeric was helping an unnamed child build a kite in the street to welcome the child's father home from a long voyage, as part of a kite-festival tradition in Rios. He encountered Cole and Maren and brought them over to help, uniting the party. All three assisted with construction. In the subsequent skill-based kite competition, they used combat maneuvers and kite piloting to help the child win with the last kite standing.[^kite-dictation]

# Session 4 development

Cole supplies fire/momentum, deliberately ignites himself, retrieves the failed spear and switches to a non-penetrating grapple. He anchors the spear in his own form and climbs the whale while Therica secures the ship connection. His remanifested form is armor with ember-edged cape and roiling fire/lightning, without visible human flesh or head. At the wreck he wants to speak with the gauntlet’s presence, recognizes it using the term **[mantle](../lore/mantles.md)** (user-corrected wording),[^s004-mantle-correction] and helps read footprints with Citizens’ cultural input. Inventory restoration remains unresolved.[^s004]
