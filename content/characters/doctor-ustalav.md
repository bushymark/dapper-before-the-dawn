# The rejected candidate

Dr. Ustalav is a prospective teacher at Glass Needle. His exact name spelling remains unconfirmed. He presents himself as a distinguished scientist whose reputation and authorized continuation of earlier research should secure the post. He belittles Elias, attributes Corvallis’s destruction to scientific hubris and claims his own mathematics avoids the earlier failure. Those judgments are his claims, rather than an independent account of the catastrophe.[^s002-interview]

His treatment of three young inventors and his preference for memorization and expulsion clash with Dayne’s belief in cultivating students. Dayne rejects him but allows an appeal to Headmistress Vesper. No reversal of the rejection is recorded.[^s002-interview]

# The platform

Before the interview, Ustalav lunges for the children’s heat-reactive invention. Poppy bites him, a struggle follows and he grabs children as he falls. Haskel rescues Finn; Elias redirects the expanding material and the white-haired child gets free. Dayne hauls Ustalav to safety with help arriving from Shamus the Grimm. Finn’s crown falls into Lower Lastings and is not recovered. Ustalav survives.[^s002-rescue]
