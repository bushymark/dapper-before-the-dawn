# Identity

Known as Sledge, he is very large and heavily muscular, with a long beard, and is the expectant father's father-in-law.[^transcript]

# Dispute

Sledge argues with the [expectant father from Eok](eokian-expectant-father.md), pulls him into an alley, and lifts/pins him by the belt. Sledge alleges that the man intends to kill his child as part of his people's culture. This is Sledge's accusation, not an established outcome.[^transcript]

[Cole](cole-buckit.md) intervenes diplomatically. Although Sledge initially refuses to release Nataniel, Cole’s discussion of strange practices in Dochas Ùr and Rios brings the encounter to a peaceful end. Sledge remains unhappy; no broader agreement is known.[^transcript][^sledge-dictation]

See [September 28 session](../sessions/2026-09-28-drifters-s001.md).

# Clarified family context

Sledge is the man’s father-in-law. He was outraged by the apparent plan for a birth ceremony from his son-in-law’s people in which the newborn would effectively be killed. The wife's agreement is reported as apparent, and her name is not recalled. The ceremony is planned/described, not reported as performed.[^sledge-dictation]

# Daughter identified

A later note names his daughter [Saga](saga.md), the very pregnant wife of Nataniel. She and her husband joined the party’s sea journey.[^departure-dictation]
