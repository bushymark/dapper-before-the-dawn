# Identity

The supplied transcript repeatedly calls this man Sledge. That spelling is provisional pending user/GM confirmation. He is described as very large and heavily muscular, with a long beard, and is the expectant father's father-in-law.[^transcript]

# Dispute

Sledge argues with the [expectant father from Eok](eokian-expectant-father.md), pulls him into an alley, and lifts/pins him by the belt. Sledge alleges that the man intends to kill his child as part of his people's culture. This is Sledge's accusation, not an established outcome.[^transcript]

[Cole](cole-buckit.md) intervenes diplomatically. Sledge refuses to release the man during the captured exchange.[^transcript] The user’s later recollection establishes that Cole discussed strange practices in Dochas Ùr and Rios with Sledge, and the encounter ended peaceably even though Sledge was not necessarily happy. No specific physical release or broader agreement is inferred from that outcome.[^sledge-dictation]

See [September 28 session](../sessions/2026-09-28-drifters-s001.md).

[^transcript]: Supplied transcript lines 1023–1089, 1117–1209, 1237–1269.

The user confirms Sledge is the man's father-in-law. He was outraged by the apparent plan for a birth ceremony from his son-in-law’s people in which the newborn would effectively be killed. The wife's agreement is reported as apparent, and her name is not recalled. The ceremony is planned/described, not reported as performed.[^sledge-dictation]
