# Session history

[September 28 partial session](../sessions/2026-09-28-drifters-s001.md) depicts Maren waking from the child-emissary dream, wearing a distinctive key and scholarly equipment, and preparing for the remembrance festival. He keeps the key rather than offering it to the ceremony. The folded cloak's cause is not established.[^transcript]

The opening was presented to Maren as a dream, whose events he experienced before waking in the present. Whether it was his own memory or another person's experience is deliberately unknown; Maren is not confirmed as the child.[^dream-dictation] The transcript's fourteen-year framing and seven/nine child-age language are not applied to his biography while identity is unresolved.[^transcript] Played-session character statistics and rules have not been compiled here.

# Kite festival

Emeric was helping an unnamed child build a kite in the street to welcome the child's father home from a long voyage, as part of a kite-festival tradition in Rios. He encountered Cole and Maren and brought them over to help, uniting the party. All three assisted with construction. In the subsequent skill-based kite competition, they used combat maneuvers and kite piloting to help the child win with the last kite standing.[^kite-dictation]

# Former Howl boundary

Maren experienced an overwhelming, incoherent sensory assault: an army-like presence spreading before him, seeming to ingest or invade him, with voices, pleas and cries. It seemed to branch through him like a tree. These are experiential descriptions, not an established literal transformation or identified source.[^sea-dictation]

See [crossing record](../events/former-howl-crossing.md). The account does not establish that these were Remnants or literal invaders.

# Session 4 development

Maren’s earth/fire personality shift defeats three remoras and supplies physical stone; the GM describes a period he will not later recall until that state recurs. He adds sustained flame to the whale and two gallons of water to Elias’s focus. On physical return, the GM explicitly identifies his victus as a bronze house key: his new body combines multiplied key metal and skin, with red light between cracks. The earlier dream’s historical identity remains unresolved.[^s004]
