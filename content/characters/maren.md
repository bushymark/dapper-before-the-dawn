# Session history

[September 28 session](../sessions/2026-09-28-drifters-s001.md) depicts Maren waking from the child-emissary dream, wearing a distinctive key and scholarly equipment, and preparing for the remembrance festival. He keeps the key rather than offering it to the ceremony. The folded cloak's cause is not established.[^transcript]

Maren experiences the child-emissary encounter as a dream and then wakes in the present. Whether it is his own memory or another person’s experience remains unknown. He is not confirmed as the child.[^dream-dictation][^transcript]

# Kite festival

Emeric was helping an unnamed child build a kite in the street to welcome the child's father home from a long voyage, as part of a kite-festival tradition in Rios. He encountered Cole and Maren and brought them over to help, uniting the party. All three assisted with construction. In the subsequent skill-based kite competition, they used combat maneuvers and kite piloting to help the child win with the last kite standing.[^kite-dictation]

# Before departure

Maren did additional research before the party’s nearly immediate departure on the Eok journey. Its subject and findings remain unknown.[^departure-dictation]

# Former Howl boundary

Maren experienced an overwhelming, incoherent sensory assault: an army-like presence spreading before him, seeming to ingest or invade him, with voices, pleas and cries. It seemed to branch through him like a tree. The experience’s cause remains unknown; no literal transformation is established.[^sea-dictation]

See [crossing record](../events/former-howl-crossing.md). The account does not establish that these were Remnants or literal invaders.

# Session 3 development

Maren uses wind to separate combatants, notices a familiar sky light and attempts a psychic attack against Swamp Poppy. During the wreck’s fall his updraft helps Koris reach the children. The shared death and cosmic transition does not resolve his earlier dream’s historical truth or child identity.[^s003]

# Session 4 development

Maren’s earth/fire personality shift defeats three remoras and supplies physical stone; the GM describes a period he will not later recall until that state recurs. He adds sustained flame to the whale and two gallons of water to Elias’s focus. On physical return, the GM explicitly identifies his victus as a bronze house key: his new body combines multiplied key metal and skin, with red light between cracks. The earlier dream’s historical identity remains unresolved.[^s004]

# Session 5 — disclosure, questions and the heist

Maren retains rope and robe but finds potions, kits and staff missing. His questions continue the search for information about the bodies and Eok’s resurrection system. He helps examine the smith’s situation and later joins Cole’s proposal to stage a historical play while recovering the bodies. The plan is not performed by session end.[^s005]

# Session 6 — preparations before dawn

Maren’s theater proposal becomes part of a split diversion. He transports pyrotechnic supplies quietly between the tent and Elias, while Emeric prepares his vampire-wizard disguise. He will perform with Emeric, Koris and Therica to give the recovery team a window. A proposed new bonding procedure is deferred rather than completed; the party instead plans to relay through existing links. The play has not begun.[^s006]

# Session 7 — escape and return

Maren performs the wind of retirement beside Emeric, helps draw away the guards, and uses gusts to cut a binder’s scroll without killing him. His cloak shifts from moving tassels and urgency to rigid, plate-like folds and an immovable phalanx impression. Daze leaves the general off guard; Revealing Light dazzles him. Maren retrieves his staff and escapes. At dawn he returns to his young, freckled human body, about 5 feet 10 inches tall with shaggy reddish-brown hair. The restored appearance and portrait do not resolve his earlier dream.[^s007]

# Session 8 — refuge and a shared course

Maren feels his unwritten memories of the Astral Sea beginning to fade. He asks detailed questions about Haskel’s living-sun account and argues that the unexplained impacts still warrant investigation. He supports rebuilding the ship alongside seeking information through the Court connection. The bug vignette is played by his player but is not established as Maren’s memory, dream or transformed body.[^s008-yt]

# Session 9 — repairs and Rustwater Mine

Maren assesses the wreck with his architectural knowledge while his cloak forms work gloves, pouches and a measuring strip. He identifies apparently growing stone-and-metal gates in Rustwater Mine. On the way out, See the Unseen reveals a miner’s presence and memories associated with Recknar; Maren accepts the wrathful remnant, gaining Architect of Ruin and a blood-red, pick-tipped cloak. He later explains his remnant theory to the group. With Haskel he scouts the bypass and the grown wooden boats, whose weak communication he recognizes through Primordial. Primordial and Whimspeak are confirmed as his selected languages in the table discussion. The owlbear encounter remains unresolved.[^s009-repairs][^s009-mine][^s009-remnant][^s009-theory][^s009-boats][^s009-owlbear]
