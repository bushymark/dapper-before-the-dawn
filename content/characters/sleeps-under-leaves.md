# The practical traveling companion

Sleeps Under Leaves travels with [Follows Interesting Smells](follows-interesting-smells.md) between Lastings and their home in Alora. She has blue skin and fur, large ears and goat-like legs. Her reserved manner contrasts with his enthusiasm.[^s009-trade]

She identifies their horned animals as Toskers and redirects the trade when her companion initially selects baby foxes: the animals Cole actually receives are one large golden-looking Tosker and two juveniles. They ask him to care for them and supply a partly used roll of repair tape in exchange. Their exact family relationship and name spellings are not explicitly confirmed.[^s009-trade]
