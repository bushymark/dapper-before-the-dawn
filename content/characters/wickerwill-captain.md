# Captain

The blind captain of [Whipperwill](../places/wickerwill.md) is transcribed as Bernard, Berner or Burner. He favors the traditional wheel, saying it lets him feel the living-wood vessel. Canonical name remains unresolved; he is not merged with Silvestro Casetti.[^s003]

Cole strikes him in an apparent attempt to knock him unconscious. He falls bleeding and is later described as dying before Haskel heals him. On recovery he asks about a prisoner and directs the rescuer downstairs to stop him. No permanent death or later final fate is established.[^s003]
