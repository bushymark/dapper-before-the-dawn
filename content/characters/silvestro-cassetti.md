# Role

Silvestro Casetti captained the vessel on which the party sailed toward Eok after accepting Therica’s request. The vessel’s name and his further background remain unknown.[^departure-dictation]

[Mission](../quests/airship-investigation.md), [session](../sessions/2026-09-28-drifters-s001.md).

# Titans and gates

Silvestro spoke of **Titan’s Gates**, reportedly built by Titans and found by heroes who fought against [Vaeldraxin (Vael)](../characters/vaeldraxin.md). The party’s attempt to recall what Titans are yielded no further information.[^vaeldraxin-correction][^sea-dictation]
