# Identity and connection

**Apherion** is the voice and named presence encountered during the rescue. A connection to [Emeric Bellamy](emeric-bellamy.md) is apparent, but its nature, origin and extent remain unknown.[^s003-name-correction]

# Recorded actions

Apherion offers help during the rescue at an unspecified cost, and the children grow radiant wings. Later, the presence warns that Elias’s attempted engine escape will kill them; its intervention is accepted. After the group dies, Apherion tethers their souls. The bargain’s exact terms and Apherion’s nature remain unknown.[^s003]

[Session 3](../sessions/session-003-combined.md), [cosmic passage](../events/session-003-cosmic-passage.md).

# Session 4 development

The same gauntlet-associated presence offers help when the return develops a problem, assisting the physical reconstruction. It speaks privately to Emeric afterward, says it can maintain the forms for about 30 hours, and challenges his public language of having used it. Emeric withholds the deadline. The gauntlet association and private communication strengthen the connection to Emeric; complete nature, costs and expiry effects remain unknown.[^s004]

# Mantle callout

Cole calls what he recognizes in Emeric’s gauntlet or its presence a **[mantle](../lore/mantles.md)**. Whether that term refers to Apherion, the gauntlet, or the relationship with its bearer remains unclear.[^s004-mantle-correction][^s004]
