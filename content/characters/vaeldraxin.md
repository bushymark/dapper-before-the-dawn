# Name and identity

**Vaeldraxin**, frequently called **Vael**, is the dragon recalled in Session 4’s Rios history discussion. The user explicitly supplies his name in correction of the garbled transcript.[^vaeldraxin-correction]

# Previous campaign

Vael was the previous campaign’s principal antagonist (BBEG) and created the **Howl**. The **Pillars of Dochas Ùr**—the previous campaign’s player characters—brought down the Howl and defeated Vael in the campaign’s final battle. Defeat is confirmed; the correction does not specify his ultimate fate beyond that.[^vaeldraxin-correction]

# Source spelling and unresolved connections

The preserved primer spells the name **Vael’Draxin**. That historical source form is retained; the user-confirmed current spelling is **Vaeldraxin**, with **Vael** an approved short name. The Session 4 rendering “Bill Jackson” is a contextual transcription error, not an in-world alias.[^primer][^vaeldraxin-correction]

Neither this correction nor the Session 4 discussion establishes Vael or the giant snail as a guardian beast. The user confirms **Vael killed the giant snail**; “Bail” in the transcript was an error.[^snail-killer-correction]
