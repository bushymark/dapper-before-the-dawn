# Name and identity

**Vaeldraxin**, frequently called **Vael**, is the dragon remembered in Rios’s history.[^vaeldraxin-correction]

# Previous campaign

Vael was the previous campaign’s principal antagonist (BBEG) and created the **Howl**. The **Pillars of Dochas Ùr**—the previous campaign’s player characters—brought down the Howl and defeated Vael in the campaign’s final battle. His ultimate fate beyond that defeat remains unknown.[^vaeldraxin-correction]

# Unresolved connections

Vael’s name is also written **Vael’Draxin**.[^primer][^vaeldraxin-correction]

**Vael killed the giant snail** in the valley before Dochas Ùr’s founding. Neither Vael nor the snail is known to have been a guardian beast.[^snail-killer-correction]
