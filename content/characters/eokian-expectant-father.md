# Identity

The supplied transcript uses variants including Taniel, Maiden, Nathan, and Nate, and includes a garbled attempt to spell the name. The user’s portrait filename gives Nataniel, while later dictation uses Nathaniel. Both refer to this participant; canonical spelling remains unresolved. The man is described as an elf from Eok, married to Sledge's daughter and expecting a child with her.[^transcript]

# Attributed statements

After Sledge alleges he will kill the child, the man says it is only for a moment, they bring the child back, it provides future protection, and his wife also wants it. The details are garbled and the ritual is not identified. These are his attributed claims; no actual death, resurrection, consent confirmation, or ritual efficacy is established.[^transcript]

Cole intervenes and gives him a desired trinket in the supplied transcript.[^transcript] Later user notes establish that Cole brought up strange practices in Dochas Ùr and Rios when speaking to Sledge, and the encounter ended peaceably, without necessarily making Sledge happy.[^sledge-dictation]

# User recollection of the intended ceremony

The user recalls an apparent plan by this man and his wife to undertake a ceremony traditional among his people, effectively killing the child upon birth. His wife apparently agreed; her name was not recalled in this earlier note and was later supplied as Saga.[^departure-dictation] Sledge, his father-in-law, was outraged. This establishes the remembered plan and dispute, not that a child was killed or that the ritual was actually carried out.[^sledge-dictation]

[^sledge-dictation]: User retrospective dictation during the September 28 session walkthrough, supplied September 30; preserved verbatim supplementary note.

# Journey companion

Later dictation identifies his wife as [Saga](saga.md), Sledge’s daughter. Both joined the party’s voyage; Nataniel, originally from Eok, would give the party information.[^departure-dictation] Name spelling remains unresolved across Nataniel/Nathaniel source forms.

# Fragmentary voyage briefing

During the journey he offered a disjointed overview of Eok. The user explicitly calls him unreliable. His claims about population, victus objects, arcane-plot resurrection, sacred fish and sky, magic restrictions, military service, and the Court’s guild are preserved as attributed testimony, with unknown scope and no direct verification.[^sea-dictation]
