# Dapper Before the Dawn

Twenty years after the Pillars of Dochas Ùr brought down the Howl and defeated its creator, [Vaeldraxin](../characters/vaeldraxin.md), a new adventure joins the Drifters and Citizens.[^vaeldraxin-correction][^therica-dictation]

The campaign combines exploration, rebellion and urban fantasy with cosmic horror in a Noblebright, partially open world. Characters begin at level three.[^primer]

Highhelm’s transported people built [Dochas Ùr](../places/city-of-hope.md), with its Pillars and democratic council replacing the old hierarchy.[^primer][^user-spelling]

## The story so far

The [Drifters](../factions/the-drifters.md)—Maren, Cole Buckit and Emeric Bellamy—come together during the kite festival. After the sun shatters and reforms, Therica sends them toward Eok to seek an airship for an investigation. Their appeal to the Court fails, and they attempt to seize a vessel.[^notes][^ending-dictation]

During [Session 3](../sessions/session-003-combined.md), they meet the [Citizens](../factions/the-citizens.md). An airship disaster leaves the combined group aware of their deaths in a cosmic state. Elias proposes a return using elemental mass, speed and a focus; the group contributes pieces of themselves and travels aboard a whale-towed living boat.[^s003][^s003-gap-recollection]

By [Session 4](../sessions/session-004-combined.md), the group has returned to the forest wreck in temporary victus-material forms. Their original bodies are missing. The three children are safe in their own bodies; a lead toward Alora Valley opens a [body-recovery quest](../quests/recover-the-original-bodies.md). Emeric privately learns that the gauntlet can sustain the temporary forms for about thirty hours, but does not share that deadline.[^s004]

# Latest state — Session 5

The party is in Alora Valley in temporary forms, now aware of the approximate support deadline. Their bodies appear in containers by the general’s retirement stage. Cole and Maren propose a play as cover for recovery; restoration remains incomplete. Haskel carries a cursed blade removed from the blacksmith’s basement. The party has promised to help starving wolves and learned troubling limits to Eok’s resurrection system.[^s005]
