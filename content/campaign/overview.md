# Orientation

The primer frames Dapper Before the Dawn as a partially open-world, Noblebright campaign with exploration, rebellion, urban fantasy, and some cosmic horror. Characters begin at level three.[^primer]

The primer describes Highhelm's transported people building a new settlement now recorded as Dochas Ùr, with Pillars and a democratic council rather than the old hierarchy.[^primer] [The city page](../places/city-of-hope.md) records the canonical spelling and historical source variants.[^user-spelling]
