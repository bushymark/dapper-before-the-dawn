# Played sessions

- **2026-09-28 — [The Drifters, session](../sessions/2026-09-28-drifters-s001.md):** child-emissary prologue/dream, Maren's festival morning, and Cole’s intervention in a family dispute (resolved peaceably). These are play dates; the in-world calendar remains unknown. In-world calendar date and full-session duration unknown.[^notes][^transcript]

The dream is narrated with a fourteen-years-earlier framing.[^transcript] The dream’s historical truth and the child’s identity remain unanswered. Only Maren's experience of the dream and subsequent present-day waking are established here; the depicted encounter is not inserted as a confirmed historical event.[^dream-dictation]

Cole resolved the Sledge encounter peaceably; neither a performed birth ceremony nor the child’s subsequent fate is established.[^sledge-dictation]

During the September 28 festival, Emeric brought Cole and Maren together to help a child build and fly a kite welcoming his father home from a long voyage. This united the party, and the child won the competition with the last kite standing.[^kite-dictation]

After the kite competition, a falling spark passed through the sun; the sun shattered, darkness fell, and the fragments reassembled within seconds. The comet was described as crashing far away in the sea, beyond sight. A possible apparent shift of several hours in the sun’s position remains unconfirmed.[^sun-dictation] See [event](../events/sun-shattering.md).

This campaign takes place twenty years after the previous one. After the sun event, the people were terrified; after an unspecified interval, Therica summoned the party and began a briefing and journey request. The exact delay is unspecified; Therica proposes Lastings in Eok.[^therica-dictation]

During Therica’s briefing, she proposed a sea journey to Lastings in Eok to seek Court of Conscience airship access for investigating the sun event and possible planetary danger. The party had not yet departed at this point.[^airship-dictation]

The party accepted the mission; Emeric persuaded Therica to join. Nataniel and Saga accompanied them, and they departed almost immediately with captain Silvestro Casetti. Therica’s prediction of House Glaren taking power remains prospective.[^departure-dictation]

During the sea voyage, Silvestro discussed Titan’s Gates, and the party crossed the former Howl boundary, where Emeric and Maren had distinct experiences. Nathaniel also gave an unreliable briefing on Eok. The order of individual shipboard conversations relative to the crossing is not established.[^sea-dictation]

After the sea journey, the party arrived in the destination city, requested access to Therus, learned of Paedan’s succession by his son, and was introduced to airship captain General Maochao. Upper/lower city observations are recorded in [Lastings](../places/lastings.md).[^arrival-dictation]

An audience was granted shortly after arrival. The party encountered Therus through an unconscious mental/other-plane experience, heard his guardian-beast explanation, was rejected and escorted back to its ship under severe restrictions. No precise duration or subsequent outcome is known.[^audience-dictation]

**Session 1 ending:** Cole challenged soldiers to duels; the party judged them weak and poorly armed, then fought into a docked airship’s hold to attempt seizure. Combat remained unresolved at the stopping point.[^ending-dictation]

# September 29, 2026 — combined Session 3

**Tuesday, September 29** is confirmed; the year follows campaign context and the matching weekday.[^s003-roster] The [Citizens and Drifters](../sessions/session-003-combined.md) meet during airship combat. A prismatic impact bisects the vessel, rescue attempts follow, and the GM narrates death and continued awareness in a cosmic state. After an explicit missing interval, the group departs aboard a whale-towed living boat. In-world elapsed time and final destination remain unknown.[^s003]

# Session 3 — the return plan

Elias proposes elemental mass, speed and focus to return to the material plane or a state permitting resurrection. Everyone contributes pieces of themselves, possibly soul fragments, and Elias begins shaping and inscribing runes. The agreed plan uses the ship remains and harpooned whale for speed, with spellcasters supplying fire, water, lightning and earth. These discussions precede the final recorded whale-towed departure; exact elapsed time and the return method’s outcome remain unknown.[^s003-gap-recollection]

# September 30, 2026 — combined Session 4

Wednesday September 30 brings the Citizens and Drifters together again.[^s004-date] The astral challenge supplies all four elements and whale-towed speed, followed by physical return. All three children reach their original bodies safely; adults return in temporary victus amalgams at the wreck, where corpses are missing. The about-30-hour support limit is told privately to Emeric and not relayed. A one-day northbound Alora lead follows, without arrival or recovered bodies.[^s004]

# Previous campaign — the Howl and Vael

[Vaeldraxin (Vael)](../characters/vaeldraxin.md) was the principal antagonist of Campaign 1 and created the Howl. The Pillars of Dochas Ùr, the previous player characters, brought down the Howl and defeated Vael in the campaign’s final battle. The exact dates and the timing of the Howl’s fall relative to that battle remain unknown.[^vaeldraxin-correction]
