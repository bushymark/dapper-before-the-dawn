# Growth that resembles construction

In Rustwater Mine, Maren sees metal and masonry growing seamlessly out of stone. Their gates and hinges resemble human construction, while incomplete growth shows what looks like a barrier forming over time. The label **living stone** is a useful working description, rather than a settled account of the phenomenon’s nature.[^s009-mine]

# An imitation lock

Elias’s investigation finds no ordinary lock mechanism in the deeper barrier. The GM compares its door-like shape to camouflage: resembling an opening does not mean it can open. Cole’s force fails to move it and dislodges stone overhead instead. A hand placed against it and magical detection do not reveal a responding spirit or magical aura there.[^s009-gate]

The source of the growth, its degree of awareness and its relationship to living wood remain unanswered. It is not identified as a Titan’s Gate, a Forged creation or the work of a named spellcaster.
