# Origin and purpose

[Recknar](../characters/mysterious-blacksmith.md) forged this powerful cursed weapon in his revenge against Court messengers. It has a broken, curved, jagged blade with a blackened snake-like hilt and pommel. It seems to have shaped itself alongside his hammering. Its purpose is killing; Koris senses a repeated readiness that it is “time” again.[^s005]

The blade’s magical purpose does not establish an independent will that orders its bearer to kill. A successful curse-lore check distinguishes its vicious intended use from an autonomous demand for blood. It is much too heavy for Koris’s temporary doll body.[^s005]

# Custody

[Koris](../characters/koris.md) wraps the blade without touching it directly and gives it to [Haskel](../characters/haskel.md), hoping to secure it in her pack. Haskel accepts custody. No permanent item name is settled: the destruction-related labels exchanged afterward are table suggestions.[^s005]

The Citizens say it must be surrendered for destruction and that allowing it into the Drifters’ hands would worsen their own legal exposure. This is their assessment, not an executed confiscation or destruction. No one uses it in combat during Session 5.[^s005]
