# Visiting Lastings

Lifespan is the time allowance Koris purchases on arriving in Lastings. The installation and queue consume much of her short initial allowance; she gives ten minutes to a boy who says his parents went ahead without him. Whether his story is true is not established.[^s002-arrival]

Dayne later uses his victus and contact with Koris to give her a week of access while considering her school application. This scene demonstrates a transfer, but does not establish every price, legal entitlement or consequence of an expired allowance.[^s002-arrival]
