# Confirmed term and significance

A **mantle** is a significant concept in the world’s lore. Its meaning remains a mystery in the party’s current story.[^s004-mantle-correction]

# Recognition in the session

[Cole](../characters/cole-buckit.md) seeks to speak with the presence associated with [Emeric](../characters/emeric-bellamy.md)’s gauntlet. Asked what he recognizes, he says **“that’s a mantle.”** Emeric defers further discussion. Cole’s recognition is an in-character claim, not a complete definition or independent proof of [Apherion](../characters/apherion.md)’s ontology.[^s004][^s004-mantle-correction]

# Unresolved

What a mantle is, how one is acquired, and what powers or obligations it carries remain unknown. Cole’s remark does not yet settle whether the term refers to the presence, the gauntlet, or its relationship with a bearer.
