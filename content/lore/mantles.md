# Confirmed term and significance

The user explicitly corrects the end-of-Session-4 word from **“mental” to “mantle”** and emphasizes that a mantle is very significant in the world’s lore. The same exchange had also been rendered “mentor,” leading to an incorrect earlier summary; that interpretation is withdrawn.[^s004-mantle-correction]

# Recognition in the session

[Cole](../characters/cole-buckit.md) seeks to speak with the presence associated with [Emeric](../characters/emeric-bellamy.md)’s gauntlet. Asked what he recognizes, his corrected wording is **“that’s a mantle.”** Emeric defers further discussion. Cole’s recognition is an in-character claim, not a complete definition or independent proof of [Apherion](../characters/apherion.md)’s ontology.[^s004][^s004-mantle-correction]

# Unresolved

The source does not establish what a mantle is, how one is acquired, its powers or obligations, or whether Cole means the presence, gauntlet, bearer relationship or another part of that connection. No generic fantasy meaning is substituted. Future authoritative setting material should refine this concept while preserving the cited recognition.
