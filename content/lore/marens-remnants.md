# Intent without a single mind

Maren calls the presences he has worked with **remnants**. He describes most of them as blended feelings, impulses and intentions, rather than complete individual personalities. A feeling such as flight or freedom might be shared across many lives and coalesce into something he can channel. He and his mentor studied and categorized these experiences; he presents their explanation as an evolving theory.[^s009-theory]

That theory connects the Rios remnants to a spiritual sundering associated with the Howl. It does not prove the fate of every soul, define the new continent’s afterlife or establish that all spirits have the same origin.[^s009-theory]

# A presence in Rustwater

In the mine, See the Unseen reveals a working miner and memories of buying shoes for his daughter. Recknar’s footsteps approach amid a tiny spectral hand and overwhelming rage. Maren accepts the presence, and his cloak becomes blood-red and pick-tipped. The GM describes it as feeling like a recently dead individual mind, unlike the diffuse concepts Maren knew before. He gains lasting access to Architect of Ruin and special permission to attune to it during the session.[^s009-remnant]

Maren has never lost control to a remnant, but acknowledges that this experience is new. He agrees to keep the group informed. Its desire for violence is clear; its exact unfinished purpose and whether it constitutes Recknar’s complete soul remain unknown.[^s009-theory]
