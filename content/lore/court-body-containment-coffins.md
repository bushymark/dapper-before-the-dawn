# Protection and access

The dark coffin-like containers resist ordinary physical blows and **magic of third level or lower**. A critical recall identifies a universal Court code: a simple symbol traced on the shell opens it. The symbol can theoretically be drawn inside, although the cramped space would make that difficult. Sonic blasts are a weakness and can destroy the shell. This is knowledge gained during planning, not a tested opening or attack.[^s006]

# Recovery logistics

A coffin weighs **8 bulk**, with its occupant usually adding **4**, for **12 total**. Haskel’s singularity reduces the planning weight to **6**, but its limited area and need to be maintained make moving all eight difficult. The cart cannot support all loaded shells. The group therefore plans to open the coffins and take the bodies themselves.[^s006]

Opening a container takes about **three actions**, described as six to ten seconds. Estimates for all eight vary according to whether one or two people can open them while combat continues. Elias intends to imitate Haskel’s tracing after seeing it; that has not happened. No container has been opened by the party at Session 6’s end.[^s006]
