# Session 2 — before the Drifters

Before the wreck, Koris carries a cursed collection including a dangerous music box. She later accepts temporary custody of a miner’s snow globe that drains its bearer’s heat. The white-haired child who carried it warms up as Koris becomes cold. She promises to return it. This explains her later search for the globe without establishing its recovery after the wreck.[^s002-arrival][^s002-globe]

# Objects in the satchel

Koris checks that her recovered objects are safe and in their places. The spoken names include:[^s008-yt]

- Hat of Disagreeable Disguise
- Book of Lost Days
- Boots of Dancing
- Weary Wristlets
- Calamitous Conch
- Cravensworth Shears

These are spoken labels with spelling not independently confirmed. The scene does not provide complete mechanics or establish that every item she ever owned was recovered. She speaks to some of them, particularly the Book of Lost Days, while reflecting on the uncertain future of her studies.[^s008-yt]

Her mother’s advice to help others and her grandmother’s advice to look after herself pull her in different directions. The loss of her fifty gold pieces of tuition is reaffirmed in the GM recap. She asks the wreck-searching group to find a snow globe, then joins them; no recovery of that globe is shown.[^s008-yt]
