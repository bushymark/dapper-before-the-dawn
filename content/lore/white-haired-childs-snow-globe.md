# A father’s wish

The white-haired young inventor’s father made him a snow globe with a small pink-brick cottage in a cool, open landscape. Although not formally trained, the father put longing and emotion into the gift, giving it imperfect magic. It continually draws heat from its bearer. The father later died in a mining collapse. The child’s persistent cold comes from this object; a conjecture about vampire ancestry is not established.[^s002-globe]

# Koris’s promise

Koris hears the globe’s sadness, takes temporary custody and promises to return it. The child warms while she experiences the cold and the father’s dream. Poppy helps carry it in her bag. She has accepted its burden rather than permanently removed the curse.[^s002-globe]

These events precede the wreck. Koris later asks for a snow globe during the [Session 8 wreck search](../sessions/session-008-combined.md); no recovery is shown there. Its earlier presence in her collection does not establish that she currently has it.[^s008-yt]
