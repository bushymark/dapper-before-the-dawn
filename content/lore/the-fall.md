# Sentence and return

The Court’s punishment, rendered **the Fall** (also “falls”), petrifies a convicted person for a term chosen according to the offense. The explanation is that when person and society are incompatible, society may change while the person waits. Friends and loved ones may choose to accompany the prisoner after a public announcement. When the term ends, the debt is considered paid. The experience is described as instantaneous to the petrified person even though years pass outside.[^s005]

The discussion treats the Fall as the universal severe punishment rather than describing a separate worse tier. Terms may range from years to centuries or millennia. Exact policies and authoritative spelling are not documented here.[^s005]

# Party implications

[Elias](../characters/elias.md) identifies himself as a former prisoner who has served his sentence, with a continuing ban on creating new inventions. A successful legal assessment suggests that astral magic might not count as forbidden construction in the ordinary sense and predicts a possible ten-to-fifteen-year term if judged adversely. That is an assessment, not a sentence imposed on him.[^s005]

Koris initially favors surrender, in part because family members are already there and she expects eventual return. Others stress that the living world and their responsibilities would continue without them.[^s005]
