# Three new mouths to feed

Cole accepts three squirrel-like **Toskers** from two firbolg traders: one enormous adult with a golden horn and golden-looking fur, and two smaller juveniles with pale tails. Their horns can damage their cages. The golden appearance does not establish that the adult makes gold. Cole promises to feed and care for them; Koris stays with the animals while the others explore the mine.[^s009-trade][^s009-supplies]

# Tape that becomes the repair

The traders give Cole a partly used roll of **Quick Seal Tape**, marked with runes. Applied to a damaged wooden cage, it becomes the wood and mends it. The GM awards Cole an automatic success for his work on the ship and discusses spending the tape for further repair progress. The full roll’s remaining charges and any precise expenditure are not established, so it is not recorded as entirely consumed.[^s009-trade]
