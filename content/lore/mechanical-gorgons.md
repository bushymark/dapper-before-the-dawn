# Court enforcement

The gorgons are heavily built flying mechanical constructs. An opening chest projects light that petrifies people and terrain. The GM describes it as transmuting the terrain as though it had always been that way, **not a beam that a mirror can reflect**. The mirror-bright shields carried by Court guards can tempt overconfident fugitives into a false defense.

They have limited flight height, roughly30–40 feet in the briefing, and are built for urban enforcement. The GM describes them as resistant to most weapons except banned material; the material is not named. Line of sight matters. No generic Pathfinder creature statistics are substituted, and they are not established as guardian beasts.

# At the retirement stage

A gorgon approaches the fight at Alora. Therica’s lasso is carried along by its momentum, then jerks its shot aside when the rope reaches its limit. The shot partly petrifies the general while sparing Dayne. Haskel later evades its sweep with low gravity and a swimming motion. The construct is neither captured nor destroyed.
