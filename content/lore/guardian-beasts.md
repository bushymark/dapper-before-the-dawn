# Celestial protectors

Guardian beasts are described as enormous celestial versions of familiar creatures, with constellation-like coloration. Their presence signals that a settlement lives in harmony with the cosmos. Binders communicate with them; they are treated as cosmic animals rather than speakers of the common language. They move freely through plots and need no ordinary food or nourishment.[^s008-yt]

Haskel initially describes falling eggs and impact arrivals. That picture is qualified: destructive crashes are not the usual way cities receive their protectors. No reliable schedule for their appearance is established. The saying that a fallen guardian foretells a fallen city is described as a grave omen, not a demonstrated automatic rule.[^s008-yt]

# Examples discussed

- **Ti’Swa:** a cloud ray wanders the marshes rather than being caged over the settlement.
- **Solm** (spoken name provisional): a city of vampires whose guardian is a house-sized bat.
- **Kailo Kolo** (spoken name provisional): a previous guardian was killed under mysterious circumstances; a replacement followed a meteor striking the ocean. Its present form is unknown.

These are descriptions and recalled lore, not places visited in this session.[^s008-yt]

# The unresolved impacts

The party asks why another object hit their airship if Lastings already had a guardian. Neither that collision’s intent nor the exact identity of its occupant is settled. Guardian beasts are not the Court’s mechanical, petrifying **gorgons**. Vael and the giant snail are not identified as guardian beasts by this discussion.[^s008-yt]
