# An extraordinary engine

Aboard Whipperwill, Elias sees a device on a dais divide into three temporal versions and recombine, releasing energy that the engine contains and meters. The explanation connects the effect to past, present and future. Elias sees possibilities for improving the apparatus and asks to work on it; Bernard refuses because Elias remains under restrictions on invention and tinkering.[^s002-ship]

This establishes what the ship carries before the disaster, rather than a completed upgrade or proof that later repairs succeed. [Whipperwill’s later condition](../places/wickerwill.md) remains a separate question.
