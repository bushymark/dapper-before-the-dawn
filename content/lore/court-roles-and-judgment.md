# Session 2 — before the Drifters

Session 2 explicitly connects Whipperwill’s captain Bernard von Kepler to Haskel’s master. This resolves the earlier uncertainty about whether the later adviser is the ship’s captain, while leaving exact name orthography provisional.[^s002-identity]

An archivist’s inkblade is described as a hilt forming a black liquid blade. It does not inflict ordinary physical injury; it can sever a person’s victus connection so resurrection needs manual intervention. Archivism is a certification rather than Haskel’s character class. These explanations do not mean every Court member carries the same weapon or authority.[^s002-interview]

# Roles and pursuit

**Archivist** is a broad label for Court members without a highly specific role, including people outside the military. It also describes specialists wielding inkblades who mark criminals through their victus and can pursue them after escape. The spoken military sequence is rendered **page → binder → forward guard → preface → legend**; some rank spellings remain provisional. The White Fang’s general is described as a “preface.”[^s006]

Haskel knows many internal practices but his own standing is not neatly formalized. He is a close confidant rather than an established apprentice or servant. His duty to supervise Elias constrains the party’s plans.[^s006]

# Judgment and reputation

Cases are described as going before a council individually, with some people allowed to remain free until their hearing. Scholarly prestige can help people be heard; it does not let Dayne suspend Court rules. The GM contrasts him with an honored retired adviser named **Bernard**, whose rare, carefully weighed opinions carry exceptional trust. The earlier Session 2 voyage identifies him as Whipperwill’s captain.[^s002-identity][^s006]

Elias’s enlarged diversion device could be evidence of disregarding his invention sentence despite his claim that he is copying existing work. Haskel’s permission does not settle its legality. No new verdict or sentence is imposed during this session.[^s006]

# Session 7 — escape and return

Bernard von Kepler receives Haskel’s Astral Sea account, offers limited aid and promises to speak to the Court. He cannot guarantee safety and objects to discovery being paired with destruction. His intervention permits escape; it is not a pardon. The party’s precise legal disposition remains unresolved. His appearance fits the honored Bernard discussed earlier, but his identity as Whipperwill’s captain is established by the earlier Session 2 voyage.[^s002-identity][^s007]

# Session 8 — refuge and a shared course

First-draft travel around age10 teaches the Court’s role and gives a more personal victus; the second draft around 16–17 offers permanent service. Heaven Sent uses identity and clearance to distribute sending stones. Haskel hopes to retrieve Bernard’s help through it, but entering a city remains risky. Haskel’s predictions of possible exile or clemency are in-character assessments, not a Court ruling. The closing GM downtime action trains and strengthens the opposition without showing a new arrest or encounter.[^s008-yt]
