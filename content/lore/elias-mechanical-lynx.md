# Adoption and control

Elias adopts the mechanical lynx rescued from the wreck as his invention in place of his previous one. In his temporary form he manipulates switches in his hand to direct it. It is called **Liger** during play; exact spelling and whether this is a permanent name remain unconfirmed.

# The Alora battle

The lynx climbs to the grain-tower binder and tackles him over the edge. The fall kills the binder and ends his persistent resurrection support. Later Elias recalls the lynx and uses it to help Dayne reach the getaway cart. Dayne grips its side and is pulled along; he is not abandoned.

The GM rules that this invention and Swamp Poppy cannot count as allies for Emeric’s bonding ability. This does not establish a general setting rule about the souls of all constructs.
