# Resurrection conditions

The table distinguishes expiry from violent destruction of the temporary forms. Collapse outcomes remain incompletely specified: there is no blanket assurance that sacrifice in combat is safe. Recovery requires **dawn within a plot, the body and soul in proximity, and the Citizens’ victus objects**. A dead person carried beyond the plot is not restored simply by leaving with the body. The covered territory extends from Alora toward the smith’s forge and stops there.[^s006]

The GM discusses capture and the Fall as possible consequences of a failed fight, rather than narrating that anyone has already suffered them. Safe original bodies and control of the recovery scene matter. A successful distraction may buy time, but guards are not guaranteed to abandon their posts.[^s006]

# Transport and access

A loaded coffin is **12 bulk**, or **6** in the singularity calculation. The belt of lifting’s first **8 bulk** are treated as free for this scene. Singularity is discussed as a **10-foot** area requiring attention to move; normal gravity resumes outside it. Elias’s mechanical feline can drag one coffin in the planning estimate. Those calculations do not establish a completed transport.[^s006]

Opening each coffin costs about **three actions**. The group changes to carrying bodies on the cart rather than all eight loaded shells. Corpse retrieval also requires collecting the Citizens’ victus possessions.[^s006]

# Disguise and crafting

The approach to the theater uses **DC 19 Stealth**. The costume exercise uses one check against **DC 20**, with a choice for each increment of five in the result. A result of **15** allows three costume choices and leaves one to the GM. The decisive diversion-crafting roll is deferred and a **+1 bonus** is granted for the proposed construction. No activation success is rolled in this session.[^s006]

The GM rules that enlarging the existing effects can be used against Elias as evidence of disregarding his invention restriction. His own claim that it is only copying known work does not override that ruling.[^s006]

# Time and communication

A warning of four and a half hours is immediately corrected: **about one and a half hours** remain before the temporary bodies begin falling apart, with dawn estimated in **thirty to forty-five minutes**. These are scene estimates, not a proved campaign date or precise elapsed duration.[^s006]

The Drifters’ link is described as lacking a distance limit on the same plane and giving approximate direction/distance to one another. Another relay is discussed with a **120-foot** range. No new ten-minute bonding procedure is completed merely because it is offered.[^s006]
