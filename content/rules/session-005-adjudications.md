# Wreck equipment

The GM resolves missing equipment with item-specific d20 thresholds, generally ten or fifteen, and some automatic results. Hero points are unavailable for these recovery rolls. Dayne is permitted a specific Religion check at DC22 for a personally meaningful object; the general alternative-skill request is refused. These are the wreck’s rulings, not universal loot rules.[^s005]

# Exploration activities

The table’s exploration rules include avoiding notice through Stealth or Survival against trackers, defending with a shield/stance or suitable short party buff as initiative starts, following an expert for assistance, investigating for ongoing relevant recall knowledge, scouting for a personal untyped +2 initiative bonus, and sustaining magic for a continually available cantrip. Sustaining magic also returns one slot of at most half the highest castable rank, rounded up, at combat’s end. This records the campaign explanation; no combat here tests that recovery.[^s005]

The Drifters’ **Rios Ready** is described as drawing a weapon when initiative is rolled. Haskel’s maintained low-gravity singularity halves bulk, doubles jumps and reduces effective falling distance to a quarter within its area. Cole briefly lifts the airship using that effect and his lifting belt, but its size, encumbrance and damage to trees make travel impractical.[^s005]

# Bodies and rest

Food, breathing and rest needs depend on each improvised body’s materials. Mostly metallic forms need less; flesh-bearing participants and the small doll form constrain travel. Riding the cart can provide low-strain rest, but Koris cannot simultaneously hide their tracks while riding, so that task must pass to another participant. These temporary forms do not establish permanent ancestry rules.[^s005]

# Curses and permitted construction

Koris’s **Curse Vessel** cannot be used in reverse to remove a curse from the blade into a person. The curse-lore check distinguishes an intended killing purpose from a weapon with its own coercive will. Elias’s restriction forbids new inventions; the late clarification allows replicating existing designs, including an already-invented explosive. No explosive is produced or detonated in this session.[^s005]
