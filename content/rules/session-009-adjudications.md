# Repair rounds and resources

The repair project uses Hull, Living Wood and Lift tracks, each out of ten. A skill’s first use on the project receives a +2 circumstance bonus. Elias instead makes a project-wide Aid-style assessment, successfully granting +2 to others’ next checks that week without spending a track skill. At the first round’s close, the board shows 3 Hull, 3 Living Wood and 0 Lift.[^s009-repairs]

A construction week and an ordinary sleep do not trigger the campaign’s full recovery. The GM ties full spell and legacy-point recovery to downtime at story-arc boundaries, usually several sessions apart. Ending a work week also ends the prior temporary hit points. These rulings do not state that ordinary sleep is impossible or that every healing ability has the same limit.[^s009-rules]

# Legacy points

The reminder permits spending a legacy/hero point for an extra action on a turn for an otherwise awkward activity. The usual use to avert dying is not in effect. This is the campaign’s stated table ruling rather than a substitute for every printed point rule.[^s009-rules]

# Architect of Ruin

Maren may immediately change his attunement to include the new apparition, gains lasting access to Architect of Ruin, and may switch to it during a session. The closing conversation identifies **Defy Destiny** as its vessel spell. This is a specific allowance for the encountered apparition; it does not establish unrestricted switching among all apparitions or a full spell description.[^s009-remnant][^s009-rules]

# The route and the encounter

Melting the bypass wide enough for Cole is offered at a cost: roughly seven or eight hours and a penalty to the next repair work, or one person forgoing a repair roll. The group rejects that method and breaks the rock instead, accepting noise and loss of stealth. No time penalty from the rejected option is imposed as an accomplished event.[^s009-rules][^s009-owlbear]

The GM offers 500 XP for the owlbear encounter after the scene ends, but no victory or that award occurs. Jokes about an experience farm are not an adopted campaign action.[^s009-owlbear]
