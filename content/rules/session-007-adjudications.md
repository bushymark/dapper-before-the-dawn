# Diversion and the opening gesture

The diversion starts with four groups to remove; three successes leave the general, while the critical crafting failure adds remaining resistance. Premature identifiable fireworks preserve two binders and a forward guard. This is encounter resolution, not a universal skill-challenge rule.

Elias can reproduce the opening gesture now, but is likely to lose the knowledge over months without practice. Guidance does not increase Calm’s spell DC; the general succeeds against emotional suppression.

# Enemy support

Maren’s successful gust can be narrated as destroying a binder’s scroll nonlethally. The remaining binder maintains a persistent resurrection effect: the general deliberately exposes himself as a minion for a ferocious counterstrike, dies to Cole’s crit and is immediately restored. Knocking the binder from the tower kills him and removes that support.

The shield guard’s anti-magic field is adjudicated as stupefied 3 for a failed save, with a DC 8 flat check to cast or pass spells through it. Removing the guard ends the effect. These are the encounter’s custom effects, not generic guard or resurrection statistics.

# Gorgons and bonds

A mirror shield does not reflect gorgon petrification; its light transmutes terrain. Elias’s critical knowledge result identifies the mirror-defense trap. No gorgon is destroyed.

Elias declined Emeric’s bond; Dayne did not. Emeric can transfer five vitality each to Dayne and Therica, bringing both to consciousness. The lynx and Swamp Poppy are excluded from that bonding ability. A failed staff throw lands it near Maren, requiring retrieval; receiving a successful thrown handoff would not itself consume the recipient’s action.

# Escape and restoration

Dayne’s successful recovery check stabilizes him before the escape healing. The general’s final damage is corrected to four from claws rather than his unreachable rapier, leaving Dayne conscious at one HP. The gorgon’s shot spares him. Low gravity supports Haskel’s successful evasive maneuver. At dawn all eight party members return to their original bodies; this observed outcome does not settle every hypothetical expiry or violent-destruction case.
