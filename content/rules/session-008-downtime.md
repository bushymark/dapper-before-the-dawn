# Food, tea and rest

Koris’s Forager feat raises the unsuccessful gathering result to a success, providing food for five. Adding the five raw sausages and using Prepare the Feast can feed everyone. Dayne’s rerolled feast succeeds, removes Starving and grants the stated **+1 AC** food benefit until the next downtime; a failed preparation would have limited its benefit to one fight.[^s008-yt]

Emeric improves Hearthlight Tea with Koris’s help and a legacy reroll. The announced modification difficulty is 28; the later praise refers to the result around 30 rather than a clear replacement DC. The GM corrects two initial statements: the container permits **two preparations**, and Aid plus Guidance can stack. The resulting tea gives everyone **6 temporary HP until lost**, plus rest benefits despite Starving. Even Cole benefits through the aroma. Take a Breath’s temporary HP can stack with these under the campaign’s downtime ruling.[^s008-yt]

Routine spell preparation and changing apparitions do not themselves spend a downtime activity. The group is granted extra ship-related downtime handling, but the actual repair checks are deferred.[^s008-yt]

# Victus and pursuit

Elias’s knowledge check establishes that blocking or changing a victus’s tracking signature while preserving its other powers is not a known easy procedure. Severing its magic can preserve the physical keepsake, but sacrifices its benefits and may cause prolonged despair. Evasion aggravates legal consequences. No severance is carried out.[^s008-yt]

The woodland respite estimate is based on the Court needing reinforcements rather than stripping cities of gorgons. Gorgon light does not require eye contact. This is a contextual pursuit estimate, not immunity from discovery.[^s008-yt]

# Repair board and enemy downtime

The visible repair board requires 30 VP across three 10 VP tracks: Hull, Living Wood, Rigging & Lift. It shows zero rolls at the close. The board says one roll per character per work round, success +1 VP, critical success +2, failure 0, and a first use of each skill by a character gains +2 circumstance. These are the displayed project rules, not completed work.[^s008-yt]

At the end the GM announces opposing forces training, gaining levels and abilities, and continuing the hunt through a downtime action. The process takes about a month, much of it since first contact. This is player-facing escalation; it does not reveal a new encounter already fought or prove every gorgon has gained the same upgrades.[^s008-yt]
