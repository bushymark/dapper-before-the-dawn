# Return challenge and unusual state

- The return challenge requires concurrent motion and elements; fire, water, lightning and earth count, wind only aids motion. The entire-group threshold is described without publishing a point schedule.[^s004]
- In this astral state, living-ship support and healing can affect the vessel; broader healing also affects the whale. These scene adjudications do not establish universal object-healing rules.[^s004]
- Cole’s readied grapple is ultimately resolved as Athletics against Fortitude with a grapple-property spear. Sure Strike is explicitly ruled not to help that skill check, after the GM revises the initial attack-roll suggestion.[^s004]
- Astral dust grants rolling twice/taking better for magical attack rolls in the described field; the GM explicitly marks this as an unusual rule and extends the effect to the kineticist. Do not apply this outside the encounter.[^s004]
- **Elias agrees to permanently change his overdrive explosion effect from fire to lightning** to obtain the missing point. This does not change all his attacks and is not confirmation that Foundry was edited.[^s004]

# Continuity and scope corrections

The narration first names only two children’s destinations; a final GM correction clarifies **all three**, including Finn, reach safe original-body locations. The adults instead have temporary physical forms. Likewise the GM backs away from the initial interpretation of repeated dawn resurrection attempts, replacing it with waiting, northbound recovery and severed victus links for collective criminal investigation.[^s004]

The gauntlet privately states an about-30-hour support limit. It is an in-world message, not a published general spell duration.[^s004]
