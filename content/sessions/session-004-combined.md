# Executing the return plan

The session starts with the group still dead in the astral state. Elias’s plan now requires rapid movement and active elements at the moment of the jump. The GM describes a skill challenge in which more elements and additional speed improve success; reaching its full threshold is needed to bring everyone, including the child without the corresponding victus. The four core elements are **fire, water, lightning and earth**. Wind helps movement but does not count among those four here. The astral whale is provisionally called [Oma](../characters/oma.md).[^s004-start]

Maren invokes motion and Tailwind; a successful Nature check gives speed-related benefits in this scene. Cole produces fire and pushes the ghost ship forward. Emeric treats Whipperwill as living, casting Guidance and False Vitality to protect it. Koris discovers Swamp Poppy is not manifested and remanifests the crocodilian eidolon, whose shadow briefly rends the spectral floorboards. Haskel uses kinesis to move the vessel, heals it through an infusion and prepares to aid the spear throw. The ship loses its broken condition in the adjudication; that does not establish permanent structural reconstruction.[^s004-preparation]

# The harpoon fails; the plan becomes a bridle

Oma collides with the ship and knocks Maren, Elias and Dayne down. Elias concentrates on perfecting the focus. Dayne produces a spectral pocket watch and offers a party benefit, then throws the starlight spear. It strikes the whale but fails to penetrate the hard exterior and drifts away with its chain. Small astral remoras separate from the whale and attack the party.[^s004-fight]

Maren changes from wind to earth/fire imagery through Circle of Spirits and an earth-and-magma spell. Three remoras are buried/defeated and physical stone is produced aboard the ship. The GM describes an altered personality and mannerisms, with Maren not expected to recall the period after the shift until it recurs. This is the narrated experience, not a generic diagnosis. Cole deliberately ignites himself to supply sustained fire, retrieves the drifting spear and readies it for the incoming tail.[^s004-fight]

The ship crosses a tangling astral kelp forest. Emeric transfers vitality to Cole and casts Calm at the whale. Oma resists full pacification, taking an attack penalty in the table ruling. Haskel’s recall knowledge describes rapid regeneration, a song that can muddle minds and stop spellcasting, and a normally calm disposition. The song is a described threat, not an attack actually used here. Haskel proposes a bridle rather than stabbing and sends Cole the idea.[^s004-bridle]

After the tail strikes the group, Cole changes the readied action into a grapple using the spear. Its creator agrees it has a grapple property; the GM resolves Athletics against Fortitude and corrects the initial suggestion that Sure Strike would help. Cole catches a rock-like feature on the tail and briefly pulls the ship into motion with it. Dayne then maneuvers the ship in synchrony with the whale, while Elias prepares the trigger for all four elements.[^s004-bridle]

# Four elements and escape

The whale emits starlight from its blowhole, and the group sees it feeding and producing astral dust in a striking ecosystem. Maren ignites the whale with persistent fire and pours two gallons of created water into Elias’s orb. Its regeneration handles the small amount of damage without extinguishing the flame; the GM says the creature is not angered by that harm.[^s004-elements]

Cole drives the spear into his own armored form to anchor the connection, taking a real injury, then climbs up the whale with Dayne’s assistance. Therica takes the chain and wraps it around the ship’s steering mast. Emeric heals nearby participants, the whale and the ship in a scene-specific adjudication. Haskel constructs a gentle lattice bridle around the whale, joining the chain pieces at its mouth. This succeeds in tethering the vessel without embedding the spear in the whale.[^s004-elements]

As the whale dives through an asteroid field toward a moon, the ship is on a collision course. A brief window shows oceans, continents and a visible world, whose identity is not stated. Koris uses a stillness/time effect to slow the meteors and buy an extra round. The GM describes her familiarity with [Ti’Swa](../places/ti-swa.md), a place where time does not pass, and explicitly spells its name.[^s004-jump]

Lightning remains missing. Elias agrees to **permanently change his overdrive explosion effect from fire to lightning** in exchange for the last point needed on the check. He rewrites the runes as the elements absorb into the spinning focus, then activates the return. This is the specific agreed ability change, not a blanket change to all Elias’s damage or an assumed Foundry update.[^s004-jump]

# Physical return, but not to their original bodies

The group tears through membranes of reality. Emeric’s gauntlet reports a problem and offers help. Amid an enormous eye and flesh-like architecture, the group falls back toward the shipwreck, with flesh forming and intermingling with their object materials. The children’s lights shoot toward images of their original bodies. The GM later explicitly clarifies that **all three children, including Finn, reach safe locations at their bodies**; they were thrown free of the wreck and were not recovered with the adults. This outcome does not prove Finn’s missing crown was found.[^s004-return]

The adults return as **solid physical amalgams made from their victus materials**, with no original corpses at the wreck. This is not ordinary resurrection into their previous bodies. The descriptions are presented as permanent-looking at first; a subsequent private message sets a much shorter support limit.[^s004-return]

| Maren | Bronze house-key material multiplied through a skin/metal amalgam; jagged solid imagery with red flame glowing between cracks. |

| Emeric | More flesh-like than others, translucent rippling skin, white/yellow light and a small red spinning core; rusted iron horns, one badly cracked. |

| Elias | Gears intermingled painfully with flesh, open wounds and a central gear missing three teeth, causing jerky motion. |

These forms’ exact mechanical consequences remain unknown.[^s004-forms] A wooden, articulated woman appears and speaks after Cole’s description, likely Therica by the companion and turn-order context; her identity is uncertain. Her relationship to the earlier clay figure remains unclear.[^s004-forms]

# Private warning and party tension

The gauntlet privately tells Emeric it can retain these forms for **about 30 hours**. **Emeric does not relay that deadline.** He publicly explains that he chose to seek its help, accepts responsibility and promises not to hide its danger, while admitting he may use it again to prevent someone being lost. The voice challenges his framing of having “used” it. Cole wants to speak with the presence and identifies what he recognizes as a **[mantle](../lore/mantles.md)**. [^s004-mantle-correction] Emeric asks for time and postpones the conversation. The private exchange is not treated as knowledge everyone possesses.[^s004-disclosure]

Koris searches desperately for her belongings and Swamp Poppy. The companion later emerges from the bushes as a stuffed alligator. She is angry with the Drifters and says her planned visit to the Glass Needle has been ruined. A dispute over responsibility for their deaths and ownership of guardian beasts follows. Haskel calls guardian beasts celestial phenomena not owned by anyone; that is his assertion, not proof of every beast’s role. Dayne mediates by asking whether everyone wants to change their current state, leading to agreement on fixing the situation rather than a settled blame judgment.[^s004-tension]

# Wreck investigation and next direction

Small amounts of moss and dozens of footprints surround the wreck. **The adults’ bodies are missing.** Equipment and bags are not resolved: the GM says possessions will be checked next session. A brief earlier reference to items protruding from the wreck is too garbled to identify recovered belongings.[^s004-investigation]

Dayne’s knowledge suggests [Alora Valley](../places/alora-valley.md), a small agricultural settlement **one day’s walk through the forest**, as a likelier destination for recovered bodies than Lastings. The tip of the [Glass Needle](../places/glass-needle.md) remains visible far away. This is a lead, not an arrival. Elias’s focus-related recollection gives a glimpse of dark, partly transparent coffin-like cylinders against giant crops/flowers, a rakshasa speaking to archivists and pages, then moving into the forest as if following a scent. The rakshasa is not named in that passage, so it is not automatically recorded as Maochao.[^s004-investigation]

Cole combines footprint observations with Citizens’ cultural knowledge. The GM explains that the group is being treated as criminals together, their victus links have been severed, and automatic resurrection is unavailable pending investigation. A broadened northbound path and repeated tracks imply visitors plan to check the wreck; nobody is present now, but a rakshasa’s sense of smell is a concern. The GM retracts the earlier suggestion of multiple dawn resurrection attempts; exact elapsed time is unclear.[^s004-investigation]

Recalled Rios lore mentions **[Vaeldraxin (Vael)](../characters/vaeldraxin.md)** and a giant snail in the valley before Dochas Ùr’s founding. Vael was the previous campaign’s principal antagonist and creator of the Howl; the Pillars, the previous player characters, brought down the Howl and defeated him in the campaign’s final battle.[^vaeldraxin-correction] **Vael killed the giant snail**.[^snail-killer-correction] Neither creature is established as a guardian beast. Maren’s attempt to relate this experience to his research fails to produce useful information. His dream’s truth remains unresolved.[^s004-investigation]

The next direction is north toward the body-recovery lead, with possible confrontation expected. **No recovered corpses, restored original bodies, reached settlement or completed combat with the pursuer is known.** See [recover the bodies](../quests/recover-the-original-bodies.md), [return quest](../quests/return-from-cosmic-state.md).
