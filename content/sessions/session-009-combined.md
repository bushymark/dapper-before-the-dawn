# Back to the sky

The party starts work on [Whipperwill](../places/wickerwill.md), still in two halves. Elias inventories the project and prepares a plan that aids the others’ work. Maren studies the structure and materials, his cloak becoming gloves, pouches and even a measuring strip as he examines the wreck. The work needs wood, metal from the nearby mine and a way to restore lift; the engine remains the difficult part.[^s009-repairs]

Dayne approaches the detached wheel gently and lashes it to his arm. Over several days the living vessel recognizes him as its pilot, remembering his help during its earlier distress. Splintered timber bends toward his hand and begins to strengthen. These older ships resist unfamiliar pilots; their association with the Bloom has helped drive living wood out of favor as metal-and-steam technology spreads.[^s009-repairs]

# Three squirrels and a roll of tape

Cole seeks trade on the road and meets two firbolgs, [Follows Interesting Smells](../characters/follows-interesting-smells.md) and [Sleeps Under Leaves](../characters/sleeps-under-leaves.md), carrying caged horned animals toward Alora. They need to reduce the number they must feed. Cole accepts one large, golden-looking adult Tosker and two juveniles, promising to care for them, and receives a partly used roll of **Quick Seal Tape**. The tape can mend material by becoming part of it. Its full remaining charges are not established.[^s009-trade]

The first work round ends at **6/30 progress: Hull 3/10, Living Wood 3/10, Lift 0/10**. The ship still cannot fly. Therica helps with food and makes Elias a drawing surface from unused pages salvaged from Bernard’s journal; he uses it for engine plans. When she asks why resurrection preserved their scars, Elias proposes that a soul’s shape might influence the restored body. His explanation remains a theory.[^s009-repairs][^s009-scar]

# Into Rustwater Mine

The group needs metal before it can continue. Koris stays above with the Toskers, and Therica watches the descent and its ropes. The others enter [Rustwater Mine](../places/rustwater-mine.md), where skeletons, broken weapons and an old camp mark previous visitors. Maren discovers that the stone-and-metal gates appear to have grown from the walls. His magical detection finds no aura explaining that growth. One gate was forced open; farther in, another is still growing.[^s009-supplies][^s009-mine]

Haskel catches falling debris as the first gate opens. Dayne spots the exposed iron vein. The party debates scouting farther but chooses to secure the ore together, barricading the narrow side passage while they work. Dead spiders prompt a disturbing explanation: creatures accustomed to repeated resurrection may fail to adapt when the plot’s boundary recedes. The discussion raises questions about the wider ecosystem without settling them.[^s009-mine]

The party brings out the available cache, described as about twelve units of ore. Further mining remains possible, but the immediate material need is met. The ore still needs refining; obtaining it does not repair the engine or finish the ship.[^s009-mine]

# The presence in the dark

As Maren leaves last, something tugs a strand of his cloak. **See the Unseen** reveals a miner at work, with memories of shoes bought for his daughter and plans to repair his wife’s necklace. Then overwhelming rage fills the scene. A small spectral hand reaches toward him; its fingers unravel into dark filaments as his cloak draws it close. Recknar’s footsteps approach, and the cloak becomes heavy, blood-red, rust-dark and tipped like hooked picks.[^s009-remnant]

Maren accepts the presence. He gains lasting access to **Architect of Ruin**, with special permission to attune to it during the session. Unlike the diffuse concepts he has previously worked with, this feels like a recently dead individual mind, full of patient hatred and revenge. The encounter does not bring Recknar back bodily or establish that every remnant is a complete soul.[^s009-remnant]

Back above, Maren explains his experiences to the others. He describes his earlier remnants as blended impulses and feelings, and shares a working theory connecting their condition to the Howl’s spiritual sundering. He has never lost control to one, but admits that this presence and continent are new territory. The group asks him to remain open about changes, and he agrees.[^s009-theory]

# Beyond the sealed gate

With the ore secure, curiosity and Cole’s promise of revenge draw the group back. Therica keeps watch above in exchange for a promised apple pie. Cole cannot force the deeper gate; the impact instead brings stalactites down. Elias learns that its apparent lock is an imitation: the growth resembles a door without necessarily having a mechanism that opens.[^s009-theory][^s009-gate]

Maren and Haskel scout the side passage. Beyond it lies a drop into a vast, cold underground lake. The walls are clawed and covered in blood, some still wet. Maren initially fears many victims, but repeated deaths under resurrection could also account for great quantities of blood; the number and fate of those who were here remain unknown.[^s009-gate][^s009-boats][^s009-owlbear]

Haskel holds a rope and uses his gravity abilities to support Maren’s descent. Maren finds wooden boats grown seamlessly in shape, two anchored and one drifting after its tether was cut. Their weak lantern-light communicates in something he understands through Primordial. Bones float in the water, and clothing is trapped inside the gate’s growth. A passing airship’s rumble shakes loose more debris.[^s009-boats]

The party considers melting the passage wider for Cole, then rejects the time cost and breaks through instead. With the group reunited and torches lit, concealment is lost.[^s009-owlbear]

# A hungry shape on the water

A gaunt, blood-soaked **[owlbear](../characters/rustwater-owlbear.md)** tries to hide across the lake but reveals itself. It walks on the surface, with a rusted chain cutting into its neck and descending toward something heavy below. A small light or stone orbits above its head. Behind it are hints of an encampment and another great shaped surface, possibly a further doorway.[^s009-owlbear]

The session ends here. The party has not fought, freed or killed the owlbear, crossed the lake or discovered what the Bloom sought. Returning to the ship remains possible. The creature’s condition and the mine’s history leave the choice ahead unsettled.[^s009-owlbear]
