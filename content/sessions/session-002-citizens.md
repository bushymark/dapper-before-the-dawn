# Four paths into Lastings

Before meeting the Drifters, [Dayne](../characters/dayne.md), [Haskel](../characters/haskel.md), [Elias](../characters/elias.md) and [Koris](../characters/koris.md) come together in [Lastings](../places/lastings.md). Dayne races toward the city on his spindle, crossing a dangerous bridge to make an appointment for [Glass Needle](../places/glass-needle.md). Headmistress Vesper has asked him to assess a prospective teacher; his children’s graduation is moved to the following day.[^s002-road]

Koris arrives by train, hoping to enter the school after the term has begun. She carries carefully saved tuition, family keepsakes, her cursed collection and [Swamp Poppy](../characters/swamp-poppy.md). After buying a short visitor allowance through [Lifespan](../lore/lifespan.md), she gives ten minutes to a boy who says his parents have gone ahead. Dayne later transfers a week of access so she can remain for an interview.[^s002-arrival]

Haskel accompanies Elias aboard [Whipperwill](../places/wickerwill.md), under the supervision of its recently blinded captain, [Bernard von Kepler](../characters/bernard-von-kepler.md). Elias has been released from the Fall but remains forbidden to invent or tinker. The captain refuses his request to modify the ship’s remarkable chain reactor and entrusts his custody to Haskel. Recovered materials from Corvallis remain sealed in the hold, destined for destruction.[^s002-ship]

# A professor and three young inventors

The prospective faculty member, **Dr. Ustalav** (name spelling provisional), treats his reputation as proof of his suitability. Three children—Finn, nicknamed Prince Finn, Tunky, and a white-haired boy whose name remains uncertain—bring a much smaller demonstration: a material that expands with heat and contracts when cooled. Their project is shared work, although they have chosen Finn as the one applicant they believe might win admission. One child’s father died in a mining cave-in; protecting miners is part of their hope for the invention. Elias gives practical advice about its instability.[^s002-children]

While the group crosses the city, Dayne intervenes when a homesick visitor, **Shamus the Grimm** (name spelling provisional), threatens a shopkeeper over a perceived insult. He talks him down before the approaching gorgons intervene. Food and help toward a Rios community outside the city settle the confrontation; the grateful visitor later returns that kindness.[^s002-grimm]

# The platform accident

Ustalav lunges for the children’s vial. Poppy erupts from Koris’s bag and bites him in the neck, and the ensuing struggle sends children and professor toward the platform’s edge. Haskel pulls Finn clear, but Finn’s crown falls into Lower Lastings. The white-haired child is endangered next as the professor grabs him. Elias uncorks and redirects the expanding material into a solid filament; the child gets free, but glass pierces Elias’s hands. Dayne and Shamus rush in to help, and Dayne hauls the professor back. Everyone survives. The prototype is lost below, and the crown is not recovered.[^s002-rescue]

The GM later explains that Elias’s timely intervention also prevents the unstable material from growing uncontrollably in the geothermal heat below. That larger disaster is an averted possibility, rather than an event that happens.[^s002-rescue]

# What makes a teacher

In the interview, Ustalav speaks of memorization, discipline and expelling unsuitable pupils. Dayne wants teachers who nurture curiosity and potential. He rejects the application, while allowing an appeal to Headmistress Vesper. Ustalav belittles Elias and invokes the destruction of Corvallis; his judgments about Elias’s brilliance and responsibility remain his own accusations.[^s002-interview]

Outside, Koris learns why the white-haired boy is always cold. His father made a snow globe containing a tiny cottage in a cool, open landscape. The father’s longing imbued it with imperfect magic: the globe draws heat from whoever carries it. Koris takes temporary custody, promises to return it and bears the cold herself, allowing the child to warm up. She has not destroyed the globe or permanently lifted its curse.[^s002-globe]

Dayne instead offers Elias the teaching position, seeing care and judgment in his handling of the children. The appointment still requires Court arrangements and witnessed paperwork; his legal restrictions are not simply erased. Elias makes the children’s opportunity a condition of his acceptance. Dayne asks for their research and documentation, opening a scholarship route for them and including Koris in the admission discussion. The children celebrate the prospect of studying together.[^s002-offer]

# Departure

The four Citizens and three children return to Whipperwill and depart for Glass Needle. Elias explains the relationship between science and magic in a way Koris finds newly comprehensible. From the air, the ruined site of Corvallis remains visible as a great wound in the land. The session ends aboard the ascending ship, before the group reaches the school. Their next recorded chapter is the contested boarding in [Session 3](session-003-combined.md).[^s002-departure]
