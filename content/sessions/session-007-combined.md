Played **October 4, 2026**, with all Citizens and Drifters.

# Trust before the curtain rises

The Citizens and Drifters reaffirm that they will support one another despite the Court’s judgment. Dayne and Maren acknowledge the value of their debates and the possibility of understanding across their cultures. Koris permits Cole to wield [the blacksmith’s cursed blade](../lore/blacksmiths-cursed-blade.md), while insisting it remains in her charge. Removing its curse is an intention, not an accomplished change.[^s007-trust]

Before the diversion, Haskel teaches Elias the gesture that opens [the coffins](../lore/court-body-containment-coffins.md), asking him not to use it to spread violence or evil. Elias can reproduce it for the immediate task; keeping that knowledge for months without practice is uncertain.[^s007-code]

# A retirement play and revealing fireworks

Emeric takes the stage as a theatrical vampire lord, while Maren plays the wind of retirement. They invite the general’s soldiers to recall his lessons and frame retirement as a struggle against administration, succession planning, sensible tea and a comfortable chair. The performance captivates the general.[^s007-play]

The diversion draws away most of the forces. Elias’s crafting check, however, critically fails: several fireworks detonate prematurely and visibly advertise that the supposed disturbance is pyrotechnic. His restraint was meant to avoid accidentally injuring anyone, but it leaves unmistakable evidence. Two binders on the grain tower and a shield-bearing forward guard remain with the general.[^s007-play]

The recovery team unlocks the coffins but cannot quietly prise them open and remove the bodies. The general spots them, recognizes the gravity of what Haskel is doing and demands surrender. Emeric tries **Calm**; after correcting the mistaken addition of Guidance to the spell’s DC, the general resists its emotional suppression. Combat begins.[^s007-discovery]

# Resurrection changes the fight

Maren’s gusts neutralize one binder by cutting apart the enormous scroll without killing its bearer. The remaining binder maintains a life-restoring spell. The forward guard’s mirror-bright shield creates an anti-magic field that hinders casting.[^s007-binders]

Dayne takes up a flaming torch as an improvised weapon and stands beside Cole to protect Haskel and Elias. The general deliberately leaves himself exposed for a ferocious counterattack. Cole’s critical spellstrike kills him, but the binder immediately restores him; Dayne’s follow-up blow is likewise repaired by the persistent magic. The general’s counterattack misses Cole.[^s007-revival]

Haskel attempts to stop the binder with gravity blasts, then warns Cole about the general’s riposte, disarm and focused pursuit. Elias commands the rescued [mechanical lynx](../lore/elias-mechanical-lynx.md) to tackle the remaining binder off the grain tower. The fall kills the binder and ends the general’s resurrection support. This death is distinct from Maren’s earlier nonlethal intervention.[^s007-tackle]

Koris and Swamp Poppy knock out the forward guard nonlethally, ending the anti-magic effect. Maren changes from swirling wind and momentum to a rigid, armored presence like a phalanx, and leaves the general off guard with **Daze**. Therica’s first attempt to intervene from the rigging fails.[^s007-guard]

# The general’s last stand

The general carries Dayne into a greenhouse and suplexes him through the glass, then selects him as his quarry. Cole keeps pressing with the cursed blade. Dayne delivers a powerful critical blow with the torch, which shatters under the strain. Haskel adds a concentrated force blast. Maren later dazzles the general with **Revealing Light**, but the fight continues.[^s007-pressure]

A [mechanical gorgon](../lore/mechanical-gorgons.md) approaches. Its chest opens to project petrifying light. Meanwhile, the general wounds Dayne into unconsciousness and hoists him over a shoulder as leverage against the others. Dayne subsequently stabilizes. As Cole prepares another spellstrike, the blade’s effect is said to trigger because an ally is dying; a golden humanoid outline joins his motion. The blow badly hurts the general but does not kill him. Neither the apparition’s identity nor the full curse is resolved.[^s007-crisis]

# Bernard offers a way out

[Bernard von Kepler](../characters/bernard-von-kepler.md), a blind astronomer recognized by Haskel, arrives from the direction of **Resolute**. Haskel approaches and communicates his experience of the Astral Sea. Bernard listens, recognizes the discovery, and promises the aid he can provide and to speak to the Court. He cannot guarantee safety, and insists discovery and destruction cannot continue together. His appeal to stop fighting does not immediately halt the general.[^s007-bernard]

The group collects the Citizens’ victus objects and selected possessions. Emeric has already taken his orb, his own and Koris’s victus objects and a box of Koris’s belongings. Haskel retrieves his compass; Elias collects Dayne’s wedding ring and Maren’s key. Koris gathers Dayne’s pocket watch and additional items, including the shield requested by Cole. Her mother’s handkerchief is accounted for in her box. Maren later retrieves his staff. The hurried exchange does not establish that every item on the table is saved; Koris leaves her tuition money.[^s007-items]

# A lasso, petrification and flight

Therica loops a stage lasso around the gorgon, but its momentum lifts her from the ground. When the rope reaches its limit, the gorgon’s shot is pulled off target and catches the general’s side. He is partly petrified and blinded; Dayne falls from his shoulder without being caught in that shot. Swamp Poppy dismisses before sharing the petrification. Therica hits the stage and falls unconscious and dying.[^s007-gorgon]

By this point the original bodies are loaded onto the cart. Emeric transfers five vitality each to Therica and Dayne, bringing both to consciousness. The general’s last claw swipe leaves Dayne barely conscious; his following attack fails. Bernard orders the group to leave. Elias’s lynx helps Dayne reach the cart, and Haskel uses low gravity and a swimming motion recalled from the Astral Sea to evade the gorgon’s next sweep.[^s007-retreat]

The entire party gets away. Cole’s final call for the general to retire succeeds as an intimidation attempt; a tear appears in the general’s eye. Whether he actually retires afterward remains open. He is left alive and partly petrified, not slain or captured.[^s007-retreat]

# Dawn restores the original bodies

About ten minutes into the woods, the cart hits a rock and loses its rear axle. Emeric’s temporary arm and gauntlet detach and melt away; the other borrowed forms likewise begin failing. As dawn reaches them, the party is scattered into the undergrowth, then each awakens in their original body. The recovery succeeds for all seven PCs and Therica.[^s007-return]

The renewed introductions reveal Maren as a young freckled human with shaggy reddish-brown hair; Dayne as an older, poised human with black and silver hair; Haskel as a featureless metallic Forged with solid blue eyes, who puts on a discarded theater wig; Koris as a small white-furred traveler with lavender eyes and a flowered straw-hat victus; and Elias as a scarred man in his forties with a clockwork arm and dull green eyes. Emeric returns as a red-skinned, horned figure in purple, holding his orb. Cole’s original armored form erupts with fire, lightning and wind. The [character pages](../characters/index.md) now show their portraits.[^s007-appearances]

# Still fugitives, still choosing a destination

The party resumes flight with low gravity and wind aiding the cart. Hiding in the mine below the blacksmith’s house is suggested, along with checking whether he needs help; no return visit occurs. Dayne’s family remains a concern. The group argues about being tracked through victus objects and whether destroying or changing them could help, but no victus is destroyed and no tracking workaround is established. Elias considers whether contributing an object’s essence during the Astral escape changed its identity.[^s007-end]

They end the session temporarily clear of immediate danger, restored but without resolved legal status, a settled destination or an airship. Bernard’s offer remains a possible source of protection, not a pardon.[^s007-end]
