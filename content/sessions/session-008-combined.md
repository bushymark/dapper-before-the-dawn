Played **October 25, 2026**, with all Citizens and Drifters.

# A small life beneath Brightfall

The session opens with **A Bug’s Life**, a vignette played through a lightning bug exploring [Brightfall](../places/brightfall.md). Beneath the beautiful waterways, the insect finds a dryad held by two guards. A mechanical construct floods the cell with petrifying light. The dryad shelters the bug in her hands as both become stone; the insect survives the petrification. The scene does not establish that this is Maren’s dream, memory or transformation.[^s008-vignette]

# Returning to the Last Ember

Back with the main party, the restored bodies are starving after days without care. Koris gathers berries and mushrooms; the five raw sausages carried from the earlier journey make it possible to feed everyone. The party returns to [the Last Ember](../places/the-last-ember.md), where Dayne discovers the blacksmith’s remains inside the cold forge. Recknar has died. The workshop’s family possessions remain, including small tools and carefully made keepsakes. Red scales behind the building are not identified.[^s008-forge]

The tools and forge are still sound. Recknar had left the party permission to use his premises and possessions after his death. They light the forge and settle in. Therica checks the road and sees no immediate pursuit. Haskel recalls that the Court would need incoming gorgon reinforcements, taking several days, followed by a broad woodland search. The woods may buy weeks; cities remain dangerous.[^s008-refuge]

# Recovering strength and weighing the victus cost

Emeric improves **Hearthlight Tea**, aided by Koris’s gathering. It grants six temporary hit points that last until lost, along with rest benefits despite starvation; even Cole finds its aroma soothing. Dayne prepares the foraged food and sausages, succeeds after a reroll and removes the group’s Starving condition. Koris spends her own quiet moment checking [the cursed objects in her satchel](../lore/koris-cursed-collection.md), uncertain how to resume her education.[^s008-rest]

Elias explores how their victus objects could be tracked. The knowledge he recalls includes temporary objects at birth, a first draft and personal replacement around age ten, and a later draft around sixteen or seventeen. A victus greatly strengthens resurrection within a plot, can have its link severed by an archivist’s inkblade, and leaves a distinctive magical trail. Changing that signature while retaining the other powers is not a solution he already knows.[^s008-victus]

Severing the magic could leave a sentimental object intact but remove its benefits, risk prolonged despair and worsen an eventual sentence for evasion. Haskel argues against a hasty choice. No victus link is severed. He hopes for Bernard’s help and knows that a sending stone might be collected through [Heaven Sent](../factions/heaven-sent.md) in a major city, with identity checks and the risk of arrest. No message is retrieved.[^s008-victus]

# Emeric and the lights

While the others set out toward the wreck, Emeric remains at the forge on a private project. He ignores Apherion’s request to speak and eventually falls asleep. On waking, he steps outside into hundreds of multicolored celestial lights and feels a familiar, comforting presence. He is awake, though the presence’s identity remains unknown.[^s008-lights]

The lights touch Apherion’s clasps and the gauntlet falls free for the first time. Emeric feels reassured that his companions can be trusted and that he can still do good. After the lights depart, he kneels and places his arm into the gauntlet to reattach it.[^s009-retcon] It tightens again and tells him not to do that again, saying that it needs him. Emeric answers that he needs it too. His project’s mechanical result is reserved for a later private discussion and is not established in this record.[^s008-lights]

# A wreck, a crew and competing hopes

The [Whipperwill](../places/wickerwill.md) lies in two badly misaligned halves, with exposed ribs, torn rooms and much of its metal exterior removed. Koris asks about recovering a snow globe, then joins the expedition; it is not found on-screen. Elias thinks he can restore the mechanics if the hull can be aligned and secured so the living wood can work. Cole offers to make rebuilding his full-time work, drawing on his experience helping construct Dochas Ùr.[^s008-wreck]

Therica asks the Citizens to become their crew and offers whatever reward is within her power. She believes their combined strength can overcome the opposition and speaks of freedom from tyranny. She recalls her grandfather ordering stone armor and runemarked stones for the Granite Guard to protect her from her metal affliction, explaining why she associates power, action and love. These are her memories and convictions, not proof that petrification has been cured or that a war could be won safely.[^s008-crew]

Dayne supports the quest but insists that his wife and two children must be considered. His wife’s letter is missing and his recovered wedding ring is damaged. The others promise to help protect or contact his family. Haskel prefers seeking Bernard’s message and a possible legal way back; the Drifters worry that surrender or waiting will leave their original mission unanswered. Contacting families, reaching academics, collecting a message and offering refuge in Rios remain proposals.[^s008-choices]

# The living sun and the unexplained impacts

Haskel offers his understanding of [the sun’s living cycle](../lore/the-suns-living-cycle.md): it consists of salamander-like beings that gather from the sea, cross the sky and return to the water. He says they dispersed to dodge the meteor. In this account they become fish, feed in schools, then regroup to rise again. Haskel believes this account and successfully recalls the knowledge; Dayne has never heard it, and the group has not witnessed the whole cycle firsthand.[^s008-sun]

This account connects the sacred sky and sea, and the prohibition on eating fish, to protecting that ecosystem. It prompts a flood of questions rather than ending the inquiry. Haskel also recalls that Bernard vetoed a vision upgrade he wanted for observing the stars; Haskel had attributed this to Bernard’s recent loss of sight, but that motive is not established.[^s008-sun]

They discuss [guardian beasts](../lore/guardian-beasts.md) as celestial protectors: Ti’Swa’s is a cloud ray; a vampire city has a giant bat; and another settlement received an unknown ocean-dwelling replacement after a guardian’s mysterious death. Such destructive crashes are not the usual way settlements receive their guardians. A fallen guardian is a grave omen, not a proven automatic cause of a city’s destruction.[^s008-guardians]

The question still stands: why did a separate object strike their airship, and what does that mean for Rios and Eok? No answer proves the incidents harmless or deliberate. The party agrees that seeking information through Haskel’s connections and pursuing its own investigation can proceed together.[^s008-course]

# A course agreed, repairs still ahead

The group commits to [restoring Whipperwill](../quests/restore-whipperwill.md). The repair board is still at zero progress, and the GM explicitly defers the actual repair actions to next time. Roughly a month and a half in the woods is the projected work ahead, not time already elapsed. No new ship name is adopted and no airship flies.[^s008-course]

The closing GM downtime action escalates the opposition: forces train, gain levels and abilities, and continue hunting the party. This is a warning shown to the players, not another battle already fought. Session 8 ends with a shared preliminary goal, ongoing legal danger, and the crew’s personal obligations still unresolved.[^s008-course]
