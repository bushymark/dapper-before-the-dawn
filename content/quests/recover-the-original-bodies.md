# Known state

The adults return in temporary physical victus-material forms; their original bodies are absent from the shipwreck. The gauntlet’s **about 30-hour** support limit is known to Emeric and the reader, but not disclosed to the party. Expiry consequences are unknown.[^s004]

The GM’s explanation of footprints and Citizens’ cultural knowledge is that the whole group is being handled as criminals pending investigation, and victus links have been severed, preventing automatic resurrection. This is collective classification, not an adjudication of each person’s guilt. The earlier suggestion of repeated dawn resurrection attempts is explicitly retracted.[^s004]

# Lead and danger

Dayne identifies [Alora Valley](../places/alora-valley.md), one day’s walk through the forest, as a likely destination. Elias recalls dark translucent coffin-like cylinders amid large crops/flowers and an unnamed rakshasa addressing archivists/pages then heading into the forest as if scenting something. The agricultural setting supports the lead; neither destination nor the rakshasa’s identity is independently confirmed.[^s004]

Northbound tracks and a cleared path suggest regular checks of the wreck. No visitors are there at session end, but the rakshasa’s sense of smell is a concern. A confrontation is anticipated for later play, not completed in Session 4.[^s004]

# Open outcomes

Recovery of corpses, restoration of original bodies, inventory/bags, route arrival, legal status and pursuer encounter all remain unresolved. The children are clarified to be safe at their original bodies and are distinct from the adult recovery problem.[^s004]
