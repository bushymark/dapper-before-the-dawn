# Request and purpose

Therica proposed crossing the ocean to **Eok**, to **Lastings**, and asking the Court of Conscience’s leadership to lend, sell or otherwise provide access to an airship. She explained that Rios lacks airship technology while the Court has it. Her goal was to obtain airship access or technology so the party could investigate the sun event and the unidentified comet/meteor, and assess a possible threat to Dochas Ùr, Rios or the planet.[^airship-dictation]

# Reasoning behind the plan

The **Pillars** are the adventurers of the previous campaign. In Therica’s briefing, accounts attributed to the Pillars described flying much higher than expected, suggesting that the world’s apparent sky, roof or ceiling might be reachable by airship. This is reported evidence and an inference behind the proposed investigation; no sky boundary or airship reaching it has been directly established.[^airship-dictation]

# Current state

The party accepted and began the sea journey; companions and departure are recorded below.[^departure-dictation] The subsequent Court audience ended in rejection and guarded restrictions, leaving no apparent diplomatic route to airship access at that point.[^audience-dictation] The party later attempted to seize a docked airship; Session 1 ends during that combat, with no successful acquisition yet established.[^ending-dictation] The object’s nature remains unidentified; “comet” and “meteor” are descriptive alternatives. Lastings is the destination; the name’s spelling remains provisional.

# Acceptance and departure

The party accepted Therica’s mission. Emeric persuaded her to accompany them with a very high roll, and she agreed immediately; neither the exact roll nor the skill used is known.[^departure-dictation]

Nataniel and his very pregnant wife **Saga**, Sledge’s daughter, joined the journey; Nataniel, originally from Eok, would provide information. The party left almost immediately aboard a vessel captained by **Silvestro Casetti**. Maren undertook further research before departure; its subject and findings remain unknown.[^departure-dictation]

# Political concern

Therica feared for her position and the leadership of Dochas Ùr. She expected House Glaren to make a bid for leadership while the party was away and thought it likely the House would be in charge upon their return. This is her prediction, not an accomplished takeover. Sygar is an ostracized son of that House who renounced his family and became one of the original Pillars.[^departure-dictation] House Glaren’s spelling remains provisional.

# Incomplete destination briefing

Before departure, the party was told that Eok has advanced technology and many inventors, scientists and geniuses, and that there are nine precepts to follow. The incomplete briefing included a relationship between eye color and which kinds of magic people can cast, “never eat fish” and “never lie.” The briefing’s precise scope, exceptions and full set of nine precepts remain unknown. These are reported statements, not established universal magical laws or a complete code.[^departure-dictation]

# Arrival

Upon arrival, the party asked to speak with **Therus**, the current leader of the Court of Conscience. Therus is **Paedan Dahhm’s son and successor**: Paedan had led the Court previously and had interacted with the Pillar Morgaen many years earlier. This establishes the recalled succession and relationship, not that the party had already met Therus.[^arrival-dictation]

The party was introduced to **General Maochao**, a rakshasa who captained an airship. This introduction does not yet establish that his airship was offered, acquired or boarded.[^arrival-dictation]

The party reached Lastings, but its audience ended in rejection and severe restrictions.[^audience-dictation]

# Diplomatic setback

Therus became petulant, refusing questions or leeway, rejecting the party and casting them out of the encounter. General Maochao then escorted them back to their ship and conveyed severe restrictions: they could not freely go places, speak to people, explore or investigate, with military accompaniment required. The party was left with no apparent diplomatic route to airship access or independent investigation at that point. This does not establish permanent impossibility, a specific legal sentence, or the duration and exact exceptions of the restrictions.[^audience-dictation]

Therus told the party not to fear the falling object, identifying it as a **guardian beast**. He described these as common in Eok, falling from the sky to defend people, and treated information about them as sacred knowledge. The recollection is unclear whether the protected people were in Rios or Eok, and whether they were said to hatch from “tears” or “fears.” These are Therus’s claims; the object’s identity, origin and protective purpose are not independently confirmed.[^audience-dictation]

# Attempted seizure

After returning to their ship, the party concluded that there was no apparent peaceful route forward. Cole began challenging military personnel to duels. The party assessed these soldiers as exceptionally weak, lacking real weapons and unable to threaten them meaningfully; this is their assessment of the encountered personnel, not a measured claim about all Eok forces.[^ending-dictation]

Seeing an airship being loaded at the docks, the party fought its way into the airship’s hold and attempted to take it by force. **Session 1 ended during this combat.** At that stopping point, successful seizure, control, casualties and resolution were not established; later developments appear below. The airship is not identified as Maochao’s vessel.[^ending-dictation]
