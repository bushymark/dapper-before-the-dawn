# The practical goal met

The party enters the mine to secure metal for Whipperwill. It finds the exposed iron vein and brings the initial cache out together, after barricading a side passage. The immediate ore-gathering goal succeeds, while refining and construction remain ahead.[^s009-mine]

# Choosing to go farther

Maren wants to understand the strange growth and his newly encountered remnant. Cole recalls his promise to avenge Recknar’s family. Others emphasize the ship and the need to avoid unnecessary danger. Haskel still hopes to retrieve Bernard’s message, but does not make that journey here. The group chooses further exploration rather than committing to combat in advance.[^s009-theory]

Elias determines the sealed gate is not an ordinary lock. Maren and Haskel scout the bypass, the group widens it for Cole, and the underground lake reveals a chained owlbear. The Bloom’s original objective, the mine’s dead, the growths and the far shore remain unexplained. The session ends with the party able to retreat and no fight yet begun.[^s009-gate][^s009-boats][^s009-owlbear]
