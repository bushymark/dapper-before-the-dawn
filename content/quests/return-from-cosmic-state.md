# Objective and proposed requirements

[Elias](../characters/elias.md) appears to know something about the group’s current cosmic state and proposes returning either to the material plane or to a state enabling resurrection. His proposed requirements are **enough mass as elements, speed and a focus**. These are a character theory recalled by the user, not proven universal mechanics.[^s003-gap-recollection]

# Contributions and agreed plan

The user recalls everyone contributing a piece of themselves to Elias, who begins shaping the contributions and inscribing runes to craft focus/foci. Whether the pieces are literally souls is questioned; amount, permanence and consequences remain unknown.[^s003-gap-recollection]

The group plans to harpoon the whale using the remains of [Whipperwill](../places/wickerwill.md) to obtain speed. Spellcasters would generate **fire, water, lightning and earth** for the necessary elemental mass, and Elias would use the crafted focus. At the Session 3 stopping point, no successful completion or return was established; the Session 4 outcome is recorded below. The source does not identify these contributions as Apherion’s earlier cost.[^s003-gap-recollection]

# Session 4 development

The return plan is executed in Session 4 with earth, sustained fire, water, lightning and whale-towed motion. Elias activates the focus; the gauntlet intervenes and the group returns physically. This fulfills physical return only: adults have temporary victus forms, original corpses are missing, and normal resurrection/restoration remains unresolved. All three children reach their original bodies safely. The next objective is [body recovery](recover-the-original-bodies.md).[^s004]
