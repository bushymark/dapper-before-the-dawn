# The wreck and the plan

Whipperwill lies in two misaligned halves, its ribs exposed and exterior metal casing largely stripped away. The group cannot simply push the pieces together and call it restored. Elias thinks he can recover the engine and mechanical systems once the hull is aligned and fastened so the living wood can work. Metal for the engine remains a problem to solve.[^s008-yt]

Cole offers sustained work, drawing on his experience helping build Dochas Ùr. The Citizens and Drifters agree on repairing the ship as a practical route to investigation and travel, while continuing to seek information through Bernard and ways to protect Dayne’s family. Koris joins the trip to the wreck; Emeric catches up after his private encounter at the forge.[^s008-yt]

# State at the close of Session 8

The GM defers the actual repair actions to the next session and forecasts roughly a month and a half in the woods. That period has not already elapsed, and the vessel does not fly. A repair board shows **0/30 VP**, with Hull, Living Wood and Rigging & Lift each at **0/10**, and zero rolls made in work round 1.[^s008-yt]

No new ship name is adopted. Proposals about contacting families, acquiring messages, severing victus links or visiting Rios are not executed here.[^s008-yt]
