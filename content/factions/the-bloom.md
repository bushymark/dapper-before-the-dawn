# Organization and punishment

The Bloom is described during legal recall as a criminal organization resembling a yakuza-style society. Its former leader and senior members are said to be imprisoned in [the Fall](../lore/the-fall.md), with sentences of centuries or longer. These descriptions are knowledge supplied in the session, not an independently examined Court record.[^s005]

# The mines and the smith’s family

[Recknar](../characters/mysterious-blacksmith.md) says its leader and a walking, flower-marked tree sought something in the pool below his mines about ten years earlier. The first visit failed to obtain what they wanted. A wooden figure he recognized as that tree returned years later, emerged without his companions, murdered the smith’s family and named himself [Tallow](../characters/tallow.md). The sought object, companions’ fate and current organization remain unresolved.[^s005]
