# The troupe’s supplies

The traveling troupe has performed in Alora before; its sign is rendered **Theater of the Mind Flayer / Mind Flare**. Its earlier production is called **There Will Be Blood Magic**. Both titles are spoken-source readings, not confirmed written spellings.[^s006]

The tent contains professional costumes, wigs, prosthetics, makeup and effects: burning-sword strips, heatless colored hellhounds and smoke devices called egg. These supplies support the party’s planned performance and diversion after the retirement celebration occupies the stage.[^s006]

Therica knocks a page unconscious and hides him in the tent. Maren carries materials to Elias; Emeric prepares a vampire-wizard disguise. No show is performed and no pyrotechnic is activated during Session 6.[^s006]
