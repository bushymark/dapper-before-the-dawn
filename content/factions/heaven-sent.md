# Sending-stone collection

A Court-sanctioned organization or shop called **Heaven Sent** operates in major cities. Haskel knows that Bernard would leave a sending stone through this service if he tried to reach him. Collection requires a full name, identification and clearance for the lockbox; a victus normally serves as proof of identity. Impersonation would be difficult.[^s008-yt]

Retrieving such a message would require approaching a settlement and risking arrest. No message is collected, sent or confirmed waiting in Session 8. Haskel’s hope of clemency through Bernard remains a proposal, not an institutional guarantee.[^s008-yt]
