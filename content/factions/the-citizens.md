# Roster

The Citizens are [Dayne](../characters/dayne.md), [Haskel](../characters/haskel.md), [Elias](../characters/elias.md), and [Koris](../characters/koris.md). This adventuring party is distinct from the wider population of Eok.[^s003-roster]

# Session 2 — before the Drifters

Dayne, Haskel, Elias and Koris meet in Lastings before encountering the Drifters. A Glass Needle interview becomes a rescue of three young inventors, a rejected faculty application and an offer to Elias. They depart together for the school aboard Whipperwill.[^s002-interview][^s002-rescue][^s002-offer][^s002-departure]

[Citizens-only Session 2](../sessions/session-002-citizens.md).

# Meeting the Drifters

The Citizens meet [The Drifters](the-drifters.md) during the contested boarding and cooperate in the subsequent rescue and cosmic transition. In Session 3, the longer-term relationship remains unsettled; their later shared plans are recorded in [Session 8](../sessions/session-008-combined.md). Their earlier meeting is recorded in [Session 2](../sessions/session-002-citizens.md).[^s003]
