# Party roster

The Drifters are [Cole Buckit](../characters/cole-buckit.md), [Maren](../characters/maren.md), and [Emeric Bellamy](../characters/emeric-bellamy.md).[^notes]

# Recorded session

[September 28 session](../sessions/2026-09-28-drifters-s001.md) contains a child-emissary prologue/dream, Maren's festival morning, and Cole's intervention in a street dispute. [^transcript]

# Party assembly

Emeric was helping an unnamed child build a kite in the street to welcome the child's father home from a long voyage, as part of a kite-festival tradition in Rios. He encountered Cole and Maren and brought them over to help, uniting the party. All three assisted with construction. In the subsequent skill-based kite competition, they used combat maneuvers and kite piloting to help the child win with the last kite standing.[^kite-dictation]

# First session stopping point

The party fought into a docked airship’s hold to try to seize it after diplomacy failed. Session 1 ended during combat; success is not established.[^ending-dictation]

# Session 3 development

The Drifters meet [The Citizens](the-citizens.md) in the airship conflict, then cooperate in rescuing children and continuing through a cosmic transition. This observed cooperation does not establish a permanent alliance.[^s003]
