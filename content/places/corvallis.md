# A lost center of learning

Corvallis was a city of universities and research facilities. Its destruction left Glass Needle among the remaining schools. From Whipperwill, the Citizens see its former site as a great chasm with blood flowing across the land. Archivists are still dealing with incursions there. The spoken label for the scar is unclear; the city’s name spelling is provisional.[^s002-departure]

# Elias’s past

Elias’s trial concerned work recovered from Corvallis, now sealed aboard Whipperwill and intended for destruction. He has returned from the Fall with restrictions on invention. Ustalav blames his hubris and criticizes his ability; those judgments are the professor’s accusations. The catastrophe’s exact mechanism and the distribution of responsibility remain unresolved.[^s002-ship][^s002-interview]
