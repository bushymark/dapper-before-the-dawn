# Arrival and pursuit

An airship called **Resolute** has landed roughly a mile away in central Alora. Other guarded ships stand within running distance of the celebration and are used for sleeping quarters. The GM notes that ships make pursuit possible even if the party escapes by cart.[^s006]

The group discusses another airship as an alternative escape, but has no settled evidence that stealing a vessel always brings a guardian beast. No approach, boarding, capture or departure aboard Resolute occurs in Session 6. Its name spelling and captain remain unconfirmed.[^s006]
