# Iron and the old camp

Rustwater Mine lies north of the Last Ember. A short descent leads to abandoned tools, skeletons, damaged weapons and a camp consistent with the visitors Recknar described. The smith’s excavations end near a rich exposed iron vein. The party collects the available ore in Session 9, while further material remains to be mined.[^s009-mine]

# Growing barriers

Stone and metal merge into gates that appear to grow from the surrounding walls. One has been forced open; another is incomplete, and a deeper sealed barrier withstands Cole’s force. Elias finds that its lock-like shape does not amount to a working lock. Maren’s detection reveals no magic explaining the nearby growth. A side passage has been dug around it, although the wall is growing toward that bypass too.[^s009-mine][^s009-gate]

# The lake beyond

The bypass leads to a fifteen-foot drop beside a vast underground lake. Ancient-looking steps, seamlessly grown wooden boats, floating bones and blood-covered walls suggest a history the party has not resolved. A chained, starving owlbear walks on the water beneath an orbiting light or stone. A distant encampment and shaped far wall are only partly visible. No boat crossing or combat occurs before the session ends.[^s009-boats][^s009-owlbear]
