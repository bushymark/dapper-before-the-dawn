# Session 2 — before the Drifters

The Citizens first come together while Dayne conducts a Glass Needle faculty interview. Koris enters through the visitor-time system; children demonstrate a heat-reactive invention; Dayne settles a shopkeeper confrontation before gorgons intervene. A platform accident ends without deaths but costs Finn his crown. The group departs aboard Whipperwill toward the school.[^s002-arrival][^s002-grimm][^s002-rescue][^s002-departure]

[Citizens-only Session 2](../sessions/session-002-citizens.md).

# Arrival and observed layout

The destination city, previously called Lastings, had upper and lower levels. While exploring, the party saw humans and other species in the upper level but noticed no orcs; they saw dwarves below but no elves. These are observations from the party’s route, not a complete census or proof of exclusion rules.[^arrival-dictation]

# Reported religious context

The party was told there are thousands of gods, in contrast with the relatively few known in Rios. The speaker and exact geographical scope are unspecified; this is a reported claim, not an independently counted pantheon.[^arrival-dictation]

# Court and airship access

Upon arrival, the party asked to speak with **Therus**, the current leader of the Court of Conscience. Therus is **Paedan Dahhm’s son and successor**: Paedan had led the Court previously and had interacted with the Pillar Morgaen many years earlier. This establishes the recalled succession and relationship, not that the party had already met Therus.[^arrival-dictation]

The party was introduced to **General Maochao**, a rakshasa who captained an airship. This introduction does not yet establish that his airship was offered, acquired or boarded.[^arrival-dictation]

# Limits

Lastings is the current provisional destination spelling. The sightings do not establish citywide demographic rules or explain the distribution of species.

# Restricted exploration

After the Court audience, Maochao escorted the party back to their ship and conveyed restrictions preventing free exploration or discussion without military accompaniment. Earlier sightings remain the party’s limited route observations.[^audience-dictation]

# Dockside combat

The party saw an airship being loaded at the docks and fought into its hold in an attempt to seize it. Session 1 ended during the combat; the ship’s identity and outcome remain unspecified.[^ending-dictation]

# Session 3 development

During the airship crisis, Dayne steers the wreck away from the city toward northern forest. Some pages and archivists fall toward Lastings; individual casualties and recoveries are not established. Garbled neighborhood and skyline names are not promoted to confirmed geography.[^s003]

# Session 4 development

Dayne considers Alora Valley a more likely body-recovery destination than returning corpses to Lastings, based on landing without detection. This is a lead; no destination for the bodies is confirmed. The Glass Needle remains visible far from the wreck.[^s004]
