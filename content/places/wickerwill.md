# Vessel and name

The Citizens’ airship is described as living wood, with a traditional wheel favored by its blind [captain](../characters/wickerwill-captain.md). The user confirms the ship’s name as **Whipperwill**.[^s003-name-correction] The final transcript renders it “Wickerwill”; this is retained as a source variant, not an approved alias. The living boat dislikes its given name and requests another. No replacement is recorded. The connection across the transcript gap is treated as likely continuity, not a documented reconstruction.[^s003]

# State

A car-sized prismatic egg-like object bisects the airship. After an unknown-length gap, a living boat is present in the cosmic scene and is tethered to an astral whale using starlight creations. The source does not explain the vessel’s recovery or restoration between scenes.[^s003]

[Disaster](../events/session-003-airship-disaster.md), [cosmic passage](../events/session-003-cosmic-passage.md).

# Role in the return plan

The user recalls the group planning to use the ship’s remains to harpoon the whale and obtain the speed Elias thinks necessary for a return or resurrection-enabling method. The ship’s role in that plan is recalled; its full restoration and the method’s outcome remain unknown.[^s003-gap-recollection]

# Session 4 development

The still-torn spectral vessel is pushed, piloted and protected during the return attempt. An infusion removes the broken-condition penalty in the scene, although the ship is not described as structurally mended. Therica secures the chain to its mast and Haskel completes the whale bridle. On physical return its crashed remains are in the forest, with small moss growth and tracks; no operational ordinary-world airship restoration is established.[^s004]
