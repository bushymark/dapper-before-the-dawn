# Vessel and name

**Whipperwill** is the Citizens’ living-wood airship. Its blind [captain](../characters/wickerwill-captain.md) favors a traditional wheel. Later, a living boat appears in the cosmic passage and dislikes the name Whipperwill, requesting a new one. It appears to continue the airship’s story, but the transition is unexplained and no replacement name is known.[^s003-name-correction][^s003]

# State

A car-sized prismatic egg-like object bisects the airship. Later, a living boat is present in the cosmic scene and is tethered to an astral whale using starlight creations. The vessel’s recovery or restoration between scenes remains unexplained.[^s003]

[Disaster](../events/session-003-airship-disaster.md), [cosmic passage](../events/session-003-cosmic-passage.md).

# Role in the return plan

The group planned to use the ship’s remains to harpoon the whale and obtain the speed Elias thinks necessary for a return or resurrection-enabling method. The ship’s role in that plan is recalled; its full restoration and the method’s outcome remain unknown.[^s003-gap-recollection]

[Return plan](../quests/return-from-cosmic-state.md).

# Session 4 development

The still-torn spectral vessel is pushed, piloted and protected during the return attempt. An infusion removes the broken-condition penalty in the scene, although the ship is not described as structurally mended. Therica secures the chain to its mast and Haskel completes the whale bridle. On physical return its crashed remains are in the forest, with small moss growth and tracks; no operational ordinary-world airship restoration is established.[^s004]
