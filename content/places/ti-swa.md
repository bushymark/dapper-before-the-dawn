# Source-backed description

During Koris’s stillness magic, the GM says she comes from **Ti’Swa**, where time fundamentally has no meaning and does not pass. The description includes the same falling leaf suspended beside a wooden path throughout her upbringing. The name is explicitly spelled **T I apostrophe S W A** in the transcript.[^s004]

This supports the place and her familiarity with stillness, not a complete cosmology, ancestry or a general permission to stop all time. She uses that familiarity to slow meteors in the return encounter.[^s004]
