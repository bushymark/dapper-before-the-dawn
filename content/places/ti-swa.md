# Description

Koris comes from **Ti’Swa**, where time has no meaning and does not pass. Throughout her upbringing, the same falling leaf hung suspended beside a wooden path.[^s004]

This supports the place and her familiarity with stillness, not a complete cosmology, ancestry or a general permission to stop all time. She uses that familiarity to slow meteors in the return encounter.[^s004]
