# Northbound crossing

The forest is named as **First Woods** in the opening route description, with spelling provisional. The group travels north from the wreck through a formerly haunted, enchanted forest of enormous cobwebbed trees and dying mushroom rings. It leads toward [Alora Valley](alora-valley.md).[^s005]

After five or six hours, the party pauses near a well and finds a half-dozen gaunt wolves unable to feed themselves. They have repeatedly starved and returned through resurrection magic. Traffic from soldiers has helped scare away game; a search could find food but would cost hours the party cannot spare. The group gives the wolves space and commits to return.[^s005]

Farther along stands [Recknar’s forge](../characters/mysterious-blacksmith.md), surrounded by abandoned building work and mine entrances. Beyond the woods the road opens into the giant produce fields surrounding Alora.[^s005]

# Session 8 — refuge and a shared course

The Last Ember becomes a place of refuge, food and rest before the group returns to Whipperwill’s wreck. Haskel’s knowledge assessment estimates several days for gorgon reinforcements and potentially weeks to search the woods, while cities remain dangerous. The crew agrees to rebuild but does not yet complete the projected six-week work period.[^s008-yt]

# Session 9 — repairs and Rustwater Mine

Travelers still pass the forge road. Cole meets firbolg traders going from Lastings toward Alora, while the party works on the wreck and secures ore from Rustwater Mine. The patrol danger remains; no Court search reaches the group during the recorded events.[^s009-trade][^s009-mine]
