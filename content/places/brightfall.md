# A beautiful surface

Brightfall appears in the opening vignette as a harborside city of ivory curves and iridescent streets. An alabaster dam filters polluted water into clean waterfalls and rivers. Floating gardens and open-air libraries stand on brass rafts with lighter-than-air gas; shaded parks offer leisure and comfort. It is described as the second most fortified center of power in the known world.[^s008-yt]

# Beneath the waterfalls

The waterways lead toward a deep shaft with elevators and stairs. Below the statues of celestial beings lies a cold prison. In one cell an unnamed dryad and a lightning bug are petrified by a mechanical construct. The contrast between the city’s ease and its hidden imprisonment is part of the vignette.[^s008-yt]

The main party does not visit Brightfall in Session 8. The vignette’s date, its relationship to Maren and the identities of the dryad and guards remain unknown.[^s008-yt]
