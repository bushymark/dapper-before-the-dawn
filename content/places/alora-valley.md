# Current lead

Dayne’s knowledge suggests **Alora Valley**, a small agricultural area one day’s walk through the forest, as more likely than Lastings for bringing recovered bodies to a place where an airship can land without detection. It is a reasoned lead, not a confirmed location of the party’s corpses or a destination reached on-screen.[^s004]

Elias’s glimpse of coffin-like containers beside giant crops/flowers supports an agricultural setting but does not explicitly identify it as Alora. The transcript variant and prior Allora wording remain provisional.[^s004]
