# Lead at the end of Session 4

Dayne’s knowledge suggests **Alora Valley**, a small agricultural area one day’s walk through the forest, as more likely than Lastings for bringing recovered bodies to a place where an airship can land without detection. It is a reasoned lead, not a confirmed location of the party’s corpses or a destination reached on-screen.[^s004]

Elias’s glimpse of coffin-like containers beside giant crops/flowers supports an agricultural setting but does not explicitly identify it as Alora. The name’s spelling remains provisional.[^s004]

[Body-recovery quest](../quests/recover-the-original-bodies.md), [Session 4](../sessions/session-004-combined.md).

# Session 5 — arrival

The group reaches Alora after crossing the forest and using the smith’s cart for rest. Despite its name, the visible surroundings are plains with immense produce fields, a windmill and road lights. [Smidgen](../characters/smidgen.md), the town’s patchwork, offers brief refuge. The general’s retirement gathering has taken over a theater area; bodies and recovered possessions are beside the stage. Eight dark containers are seen, with a hand from one of the party’s bodies visible. The village is now reached, but the recovery is incomplete. “Alora / Allora” spelling remains provisional.[^s005]

# Session 6 — preparations before dawn

The rural-looking settlement is described as vast despite having no obvious valley. The retirement stage is an open-air auditorium with upright coffins, nearby guards and ritual-working binders; shrubs are reshaped into skeletal forms. A grain-tower bell announces approaching dawn using notice from the east. Therica reports a throne-of-bees contest for the mayor. These local customs do not explain how dawn is known in advance. The party raids [the theater’s supply tent](../factions/theater-of-the-mind-flayer.md), but has not performed its diversion or recovered a body.[^s006]
