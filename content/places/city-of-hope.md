# Canonical name

**Dochas Ùr**, the City of Hope, is the settlement built by Highhelm’s transported people.[^user-spelling]

The city’s name is also written Dochas Ur and Dochus Ur in older accounts.[^primer][^bio]

# The city

The primer connects the city's foundation to the transported people of Highhelm, and describes Pillars and a democratic council.[^primer] Maren's biography places his later upbringing and hospital treatment in the city.[^bio]

# September 28 setting narration

Travelers encounter a patchwork city that built upward to avoid creating an excluded outside-wall district, and a remembrance festival welcoming returning travelers. Some hoard/material terms are garbled and remain unresolved.[^transcript] See [festival custom](../events/remembrance-festival.md) and [session recap](../sessions/2026-09-28-drifters-s001.md).

# Session 6 — preparations before dawn

The group offers a possible home in Rios if the Citizens lose their place in Eok, recalling that a Forged was among Dochas Ùr’s founding Pillars. Haskel is willing to consider refuge only if it does not bring war. Therica’s authority is discussed alongside the realization that the party may have been away for more than a month. Whether House Glaren has taken power remains unknown; no asylum decision or return occurs.[^s006]
