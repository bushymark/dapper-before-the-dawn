# Canonical name

The canonical campaign spelling is **Dochas Ùr**. On 2026-09-30 the user confirmed Dochas rather than Dochus and specified the Scottish Gaelic accent on the U; this is the grave accent **Ù**.[^user-spelling]

The primer prints **Dochas Ur**, glossed as City of Hope.[^primer] The finalized Maren biography prints **Dochus Ur**.[^bio] Retain those original source spellings in direct quotations and source records; use **Dochas Ùr** in curated narration. The earlier spelling conflict is resolved by the user's correction.

The stable entity path remains unchanged. The unaccented form and former misspelling remain registry aliases for recognizing historical references; aliases do not make the old spelling canonical. The Gaelic word for hope is normally written dòchas, but adding an accent to the first word of the campaign name has not been confirmed by the user.

# Source-supported setting

The primer connects the city's foundation to the transported people of Highhelm, and describes Pillars and a democratic council.[^primer] Maren's biography places his later upbringing and hospital treatment in the city.[^bio]
