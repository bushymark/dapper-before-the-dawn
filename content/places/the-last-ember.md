# The abandoned workshop

The party returns to Recknar’s forge, called **the Last Ember**, after escaping Alora. Dayne discovers bone ash inside the forge; the smith has ended his life there. Dayne does not immediately draw the others’ attention to the remains.[^s008-yt]

The forge is cold and dusty but sound, with usable tools, tongs, firebrick and cookware. It burns wood and coal. Family traces remain: two adult aprons and a smaller one, a child-sized hammer, a carefully repaired copper bracelet and small iron birds. Dayne notices red creature scales behind the workshop but cannot identify them.[^s008-yt]

# Refuge and permission

Recknar had explicitly left the premises and supplies for the party to use, answering their concern about taking his possessions under the fourth precept. The group lights the forge, makes tea and food, rests, and considers it a base while restoring Whipperwill. No current mining expedition or exploration of the basement occurs.[^s008-yt]

Therica sees no immediate pursuers. Haskel’s knowledge assessment suggests that reinforcements and a broad woodland search buy time, while entering a city would be much riskier. This is an assessment, not guaranteed safety.[^s008-yt]

# Session 9 — repairs and Rustwater Mine

The forge stands among unfinished buildings that Recknar hoped would form a community; the road still has passing travelers. The party uses the area as its repair base and goes north to Rustwater Mine for iron. Therica gathers food and helps Elias plan the engine. No new permanent settlement is completed.[^s009-trade][^s009-scar][^s009-mine]
