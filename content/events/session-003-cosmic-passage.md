# Narrated experience

Following Elias’s fallback engine escape, the characters’ bodies dissolve and the GM explicitly narrates their awareness of having died. Imagery includes a red gemstone, an immense wax tree with candles, and a beach-like star-filled state without ordinary shape or mass. [Apherion](../characters/apherion.md) tethers souls. The user identifies the voice and named presence as Apherion and notes an apparent connection to Emeric; the nature of that connection remains unresolved.[^s003-name-correction][^s003]

Seven forms appear: gauntlet, tasselled cloak, armor, hat, compass, ring and inscribed clockwork object. The GM adds four initially omitted forms: crown, snow globe, little frog statue and red-haired clay woman. Individual identities are not assigned without additional evidence. Movement and thought are described; ordinary speech is not established there. This is not confirmation of permanent death or normal resurrection.[^s003]

# Recording gap and departure

Line 4975 explicitly marks an unknown-length gap. The user later supplies the planning and contributions during that gap; this is retrospective evidence rather than recovered transcript.[^s003-gap-recollection] When the transcript resumes, a living boat and astral whale are already present. Dayne shapes a starlight harpoon; Haskel makes a starlight chain, and the whale tows the boat across the cosmic sky. The living boat wants the name Whipperwill changed. Its acquisition/restoration, the whale’s discovery, the destination and return to ordinary bodies are not supplied.[^s003]

# Plan within the gap

In the user’s recollection, Elias proposes elemental mass, speed and focus as a means of returning the group to the material plane or a state permitting resurrection. Everyone contributes pieces of themselves, possibly soul fragments, and Elias begins shaping and rune-inscribing the focus/foci. The group plans to harpoon the whale using the remains of the ship for speed and have spellcasters generate fire, water, lightning and earth. No successful return is reported.[^s003-gap-recollection]
