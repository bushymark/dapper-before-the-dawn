# Narrated experience

Following Elias’s fallback engine escape, the characters’ bodies dissolve and each knows they have died. They see a red gemstone, an immense wax tree with candles, and a beach-like, star-filled expanse without ordinary shape or mass. [Apherion](../characters/apherion.md) tethers their souls; an apparent connection to Emeric remains unexplained.[^s003-name-correction][^s003]

Seven forms appear: gauntlet, tasselled cloak, armor, hat, compass, ring and inscribed clockwork object. The GM adds four initially omitted forms: crown, snow globe, little frog statue and red-haired clay woman. Individual identities are not assigned without additional evidence. Movement and thought are described; ordinary speech is not established there. This is not confirmation of permanent death or normal resurrection.[^s003]

# The whale-towed voyage

Later, a living boat and astral whale are present. Dayne shapes a starlight harpoon and Haskel makes a starlight chain; the whale tows the boat across the cosmic sky. The boat wants its name, Whipperwill, changed. How it was recovered and how the whale was found remain unknown.[^s003-gap-recollection][^s003]

[Airship disaster](session-003-airship-disaster.md), [session](../sessions/session-003-combined.md).

# Plan within the gap

Elias proposes elemental mass, speed and focus as a way to return to the material plane or a state permitting resurrection. Everyone contributes pieces of themselves, perhaps soul fragments, and Elias shapes and inscribes runes on a focus. The plan uses the ship’s remains and a harpooned whale for speed, while spellcasters provide fire, water, lightning and earth. Its outcome is not yet known at the end of Session 3.[^s003-gap-recollection]

[Return plan](../quests/return-from-cosmic-state.md).

# Session 4 development

The later Session 4 resolves the next transition: all four elements and whale-towed motion trigger a physical return. The adults land at the wreck in temporary victus-material forms, not at their missing corpses; all three children are clarified safe at their own bodies. See [physical return](session-004-physical-return.md). Some details of the earlier passage remain unknown.[^s004]
