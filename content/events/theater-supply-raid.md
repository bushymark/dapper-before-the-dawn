# A quiet approach and one witness

Koris, Maren, Emeric and Therica approach the theater supply tent. A page rises on seeing them; Therica strikes him with a chair, renders him unconscious and conceals him under wigs and a dress. He is not killed. The group finds professional costumes and theatrical effects.[^s006]

Maren transports the effects quietly to Elias, while Emeric prepares a vampire-wizard costume for him. Elias’s larger diversion is assembled with Cole’s help and placed with Koris. Its decisive roll and activation are deferred. This raid does not mean the stage performance, body rescue or confrontation with the general has occurred.[^s006]
