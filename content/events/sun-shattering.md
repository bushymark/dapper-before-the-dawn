# Event as presented

After the kite competition, a falling spark passed through the sun. In the scene as described to the party, the sun shattered into thousands of pieces across the sky, darkening the land. The object, also described as a comet, continued toward a distant crash in the sea beyond the party’s sight. Within a couple of seconds the sun’s fragments reassembled and daylight returned.[^sun-dictation]

# Possible positional shift

The reformed sun may have appeared farther along its path, as though several hours had passed in seconds. That apparent movement remains unconfirmed; neither an actual passage of hours nor its cause is known.[^sun-dictation]

# Unresolved

The object’s nature, cause of the sun’s fragmentation and recovery, and exact sea impact location are unknown. The distant crash was described as beyond the party’s sight; no visit to or direct inspection of the impact is reported. “The sun shatters and reforms” is a descriptive page title, not an established in-world event name.

# Public response

The people were terrified after the sun event. Rios’s people are familiar with fantastical events and world-ending threats, and take such threats very seriously; the current campaign is twenty years after Campaign 1.[^therica-dictation]

Unease followed the spectacle. The party was later summoned by [Therica](../characters/therica-ashtongue.md); the exact interval remains unknown.[^therica-dictation]

# Proposed investigation

Therica proposed obtaining airship access from the Court of Conscience in Eok to investigate the sun event and the unidentified object, and assess possible danger to Dochas Ùr, Rios or the planet. This is an investigation goal, not confirmation of a particular threat or cause.[^airship-dictation] See [mission](../quests/airship-investigation.md).

# Therus’s explanation

Therus told the party not to fear the falling object, identifying it as a **guardian beast**. He described these as common in Eok, falling from the sky to defend people, and treated information about them as sacred knowledge. The recollection is unclear whether the protected people were in Rios or Eok, and whether they were said to hatch from “tears” or “fears.” These are Therus’s claims; the object’s identity, origin and protective purpose are not independently confirmed.[^audience-dictation]

The earlier unidentified-object record is retained: this explanation is testimony, not independent confirmation. See [audience](therus-audience.md).
