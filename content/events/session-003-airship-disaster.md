# Observed event

A car-sized, prismatic egg-like object cuts [Whipperwill](../places/wickerwill.md) in two. The ship and separated passenger groups begin to fall. This follows the [seizure attempt](airship-seizure-attempt.md); the narration does not establish a successfully acquired operational airship.[^s003]

Dayne redirects the wreck away from the city toward northern forest. Cole drops weapons and turns toward saving lives; Maren helps Koris reach the children with an updraft. The children grow radiant feathered wings after help is accepted. Elias attempts an engine-powered escape, but the intended time-jump lacks sufficient power and a fallback teleportation triggers.[^s003]

# Limits

Recalled lore associates celestial guardian-beast arrivals with new cities, but neither the impact’s intent nor its connection to the earlier [sun event](sun-shattering.md) is proven. Individual falling soldiers’ fates, the missing prince’s crown, intervention identity and cost remain unresolved.[^s003]

The sequence leads into [death and cosmic passage](session-003-cosmic-passage.md). [Full recap](../sessions/session-003-combined.md).

# Session 4 development

Session 4’s final GM clarification says all three children were thrown free of the wreck, were not recovered with the adults, and return safely to their original bodies. The adults’ corpses are absent when their temporary forms remanifest at the wreck. This does not resolve individual military casualties or recover Finn’s crown.[^s004]
