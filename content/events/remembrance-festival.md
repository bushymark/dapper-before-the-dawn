# Described practices

The narration describes a general remembrance holiday held when there is a reason to remember, rather than a fixed calendar observance. On this occasion people are returning from travels in Eok, and residents make and fly colorful kites to guide and welcome them home.[^transcript]

Residents contribute sentimental metal objects, optionally with ashes of loved ones, to be forged into weapons. At the day's end those weapons are repurposed into practical objects requested by people who need them. The narration connects this to preserving the city's ability to arm itself.[^transcript]

A dwarven master of ceremonies and his daughter check whether anyone needs help or cannot attend. Their names are garbled; no canonical spelling is yet established.[^transcript]

# Personal significance

Maren keeps his distinctive key on a chain rather than giving it up for the ceremony, despite prior encouragement to do so.[^transcript] It remains unclear the key's owner, what it opens, or its full significance.

# Official name and date

The festival’s official title and its in-world calendar date remain unknown. “Remembrance festival” is a descriptive name.

# Kite competition and party assembly

Emeric was helping an unnamed child build a kite in the street to welcome the child's father home from a long voyage, as part of a kite-festival tradition in Rios. He encountered Cole and Maren and brought them over to help, uniting the party. All three assisted with construction. In the subsequent skill-based kite competition, they used combat maneuvers and kite piloting to help the child win with the last kite standing.[^kite-dictation]

The child’s and father’s names, specific rolls, mechanical abilities and any prize remain unspecified.

# After the kite competition

The sun briefly shattered and reformed after a spark crossed it, plunging the land into darkness for seconds. See [the event record](sun-shattering.md), including the unconfirmed recollection of a changed solar position.[^sun-dictation]
