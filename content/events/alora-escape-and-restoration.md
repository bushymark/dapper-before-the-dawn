# Recovery under pressure

The retirement play draws off most guards but fails to conceal the recovery team completely. The coffins are unlocked, and the original bodies are loaded onto the cart during the encounter. All eight originals are present at departure; the Citizens’ victus objects and selected belongings are retrieved.

Bernard offers limited help, Therica’s lasso diverts the gorgon onto the general, and the party escapes. The general remains alive and partly petrified. One binder dies in a fall; the other binder and shield guard are neutralized nonlethally. Their later resurrection or recovery is not shown.

# Dawn in the woods

Roughly ten minutes into the woods, the cart strikes a rock and loses its rear axle. The temporary forms collapse as dawn arrives. Each of the seven PCs and Therica awakens in their original body. This completes the immediate body-recovery objective; it does not erase the Court’s charges or resolve the investigation of the sky event.

# Continuing flight

After work on its damaged wheel, the group resumes the cart’s journey, aided by low gravity and wind. Refuge at the blacksmith’s mine and helping him are proposed but not carried out before play closes. The party is temporarily free of immediate pursuit.
