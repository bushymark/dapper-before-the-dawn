# Sequence and stopping point

After returning to their ship, the party concluded that there was no apparent peaceful route forward. Cole began challenging military personnel to duels. The party assessed these soldiers as exceptionally weak, lacking real weapons and unable to threaten them meaningfully; this is their assessment of the encountered personnel, not a measured claim about all Eok forces.[^ending-dictation]

Seeing an airship being loaded at the docks, the party fought its way into the airship’s hold and attempted to take it by force. **Session 1 ended during this combat.** At that stopping point, successful seizure, control, casualties and resolution were not established; later developments are recorded below. The airship is not identified as Maochao’s vessel.[^ending-dictation]
