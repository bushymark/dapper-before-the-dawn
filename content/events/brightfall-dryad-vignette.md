# A small witness

A lightning bug explores Brightfall’s waterfalls and descends into an underground cell. There, two guards hold a dryad with mahogany-colored skin, leafy hair and emerald eyes. One guard repeats a warning that her influence can corrupt a man’s mind; that accusation remains attributed to him. The person named in the warning is rendered “Odra,” with identity and spelling unresolved.[^s008-yt]

A winged mechanical creature opens its chest and fills the cell with painful petrifying light. The dryad tries to protect the insect, sheltering it with her hands as her own body turns to stone. The bug reaches her finger. Both are left petrified, and the GM explicitly clarifies that the insect survives rather than dies. The guards leave them in darkness.[^s008-yt]

# Limits of the scene

The GM presents this as a short opening vignette played through a bug by Maren’s player. No connection is established to Maren’s dream, his personal memories, the main party’s location or the dryad’s identity. It is not evidence that Maren himself became an insect.[^s008-yt]
