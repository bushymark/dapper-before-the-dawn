# Encounter

The audience was granted almost immediately. The party was led to the strange ship where Therus resided. He was wheeled out in a black cube, with a fetus-like figure visible at its center. The party was knocked unconscious and then communicated with him in what appeared to be another plane or a mental space; its nature is unknown. There, he appeared as an angelic winged being. His presentation does not establish an angelic ancestry or that the fetus-like image describes his literal developmental state.[^audience-dictation]

# Family and leadership account

During the conversation, the party understood Therus to be a child who hated his parents and Rios, resented Paedan’s affection for Rios, and disliked the party. They learned that his parents were serving time and that he had turned them in, condemning them before becoming the Court’s de facto leader. This records the information obtained and the party’s assessment during the audience; the charges, sentence, exact age and parents’ own account are not supplied.[^audience-dictation]

# Guardian-beast claims

Therus told the party not to fear the falling object, identifying it as a **guardian beast**. He described these as common in Eok, falling from the sky to defend people, and treated information about them as sacred knowledge. The recollection is unclear whether the protected people were in Rios or Eok, and whether they were said to hatch from “tears” or “fears.” These are Therus’s claims; the object’s identity, origin and protective purpose are not independently confirmed.[^audience-dictation]

# Mental-link access

Therus could hear the party’s conversations through their shared mental link during the encounter. Whether he could also hear that link outside the strange encounter space is explicitly unknown; no persistent surveillance or universal telepathic ability is inferred.[^audience-dictation]

# Rejection and restrictions

The user recalls Therus becoming petulant, refusing questions or leeway, rejecting the party and casting them out of the encounter. General Maochao then escorted them back to their ship and conveyed severe restrictions: they could not freely go places, speak to people, explore or investigate, with military accompaniment required. The party was left with no apparent diplomatic route to airship access or independent investigation at that point. This does not establish permanent impossibility, a specific legal sentence, or the duration and exact exceptions of the restrictions.[^audience-dictation]
