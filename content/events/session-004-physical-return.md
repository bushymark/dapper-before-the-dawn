# Result of the return attempt

After all four elements and whale-towed motion are assembled, Elias activates the focus. The group passes through strange planes and the gauntlet offers further intervention. Adults remanifest physically at the shipwreck as amalgams of flesh and victus materials, with no original corpses there. This is a new physical return, not ordinary resurrection into the missing bodies.[^s004]

All three children depart toward their own bodies and are later explicitly clarified to be safe there. Their bodies were thrown clear of the wreck and not collected with the adults. Finn’s missing crown is not reported recovered.[^s004]

# Private limit

The gauntlet tells Emeric privately that it can maintain the forms for **about 30 hours**. He does not relay that limit. Earlier permanent-looking descriptions are superseded as a duration claim by this private statement; exact expiry consequences remain unknown.[^s004]

# Session 7 — escape and return

The provisional victus-material forms from Session 4 finally fail as the party escapes Alora at dawn in Session 7. With the original bodies recovered and nearby, all eight awaken in those originals. The successful return resolves this immediate temporary-body episode; it does not establish every hypothetical failure case.[^s007]
